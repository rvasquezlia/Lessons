🚀 Course Overview

In Grade 7 Pre-Algebra, instructional time focuses on four critical mathematical areas: developing understanding of and applying proportional relationships, operations with rational numbers and linear equations, scale drawings and geometric analysis, and drawing statistical inferences.

We will work through abstract reasoning, models, and problem-solving strategies to build algebra readiness. To achieve your best potential, a highly organized notebook is required for this course.

🧠 What We Will Learn

🔢 The Number System

Rational Numbers Integer Operations Factors & Fractions

✖️ Expressions & Equations

📈 Ratios & Proportions

Ratio & Proportion Percent Applications Rates
