🚀 Course Overview

This 6th Grade Math course is the final course in basic arithmetic offered at LIA. It provides the core concepts needed to move on and be ready for Pre-Algebra. Through critical thinking, you will record, represent, and solve real-world problems!

We will use models, manipulatives, quick pictures, and symbols to build mathematical understanding. To reach your maximal potential, a highly organized notebook is required for this course.
