Vocabulary: Reading the Clues in a Word Problem
Word problems don't say "add" or "subtract" - they hide it in the words. Two questions to ask every time: (1) Which operation do the words point to? and (2) Does the number represent a gain (positive) or a loss (negative)?

Title: Integer Word Problem Vocabulary

  • Positive (+) clues: gain, rise, increase, deposit, profit, above, up, forward, earn.
  • Negative (-) clues: lose, drop, decrease, withdraw, debt, below, down, backward, owe, spend.
  • When two changes happen one after another (a gain then a loss), you usually add the signed values in order.
  • When the same change repeats several times (the same loss or gain each day, hour, or trip), you multiply.
  • When a total change is split evenly into equal parts, you divide.

Which Operation Do the Words Point To?
OperationKeywords to look forWhy
Addition (+) sum, total, altogether, combined, in all, increased by, gained, rose, more than Two or more changes are being put together.
Subtraction (-) difference, less than, decreased by, fewer, dropped, fell, how much more/farther, remaining You're finding a change, a gap, or what's left.
Multiplication (×) each, every, per, times, groups of, "the same … for 3 days/hours/trips" The same signed change repeats several times.
Division (÷) split evenly, shared equally, divided into, per unit, average change, "each of the …" One total change is broken into equal parts.
Is the Number Positive or Negative?
SignKeywords / real-life meaningExample
Positive (+) gain, deposit, profit, earn, rise, increase, above sea level/zero, climb, forward "Deposits \(\$40\)" → \(+40\)
Negative (-) lose, withdraw, debt, owe, spend, drop, decrease, below sea level/zero, descend, backward "Withdraws \(\$15\)" → \(-15\)
Quick Check

Say whether each phrase is a gain (positive) or a loss (negative).

1. "The hiker's elevation dropped 30 feet." Type positive or negative.
2. "She deposited \(\$20\) into her account." Type positive or negative.
3. "The team loses the same 5 yards on each of 3 plays." Which operation do the words point to - add, subtract, multiply, or divide?
