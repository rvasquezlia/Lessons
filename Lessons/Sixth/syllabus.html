Course Overview
Sixth Grade Math is the final course in basic arithmetic offered at LIA. It provides the core concepts needed to move on and be ready for Pre-Algebra. Through critical thinking, you will record, represent, and solve real-world problems using models, manipulatives, quick pictures, and symbols.
Notebook Requirement

A highly organized math notebook is required for this course. You will copy key vocabulary, worked examples, and reference charts from the lesson pages into your notebook every week — it is your most important study tool for tests and the quarter exam.
