Independent Practice Batches

For the teacher Print and hand out Batch 1 to half the class and Batch 2 to the other half (alternating, by table, however works for your roster). For Peer Review, pair a Batch 1 student with a Batch 2 student. Answer key is in the Teacher Guide's Digital Answer Key tab.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Batch 1

Solve each problem below. Show your work in the space provided.

Word Problems

1. A book costs \(\$12.99\). If you buy \(3\) books, what is the total cost before tax?

2. You have \(\$25.00\). You spend \(\$8.45\) on lunch. How much do you have left?

3. A school club buys \(8\) notebooks at \(\$1.75\) each for a fundraiser. What is the total cost?

4. A jug contains \(2.5\) liters of juice. If \(0.6\) liters is poured out, how much juice remains?
