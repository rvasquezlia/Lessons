Real-World Decimal Jigsaw: Group Cards

For the teacher Print one section per table (cut apart or photocopy separately) and hand each table only their theme's card. Students solve on this card, then type and check their own answers on the class page (which matches exactly). Answer key is in the Teacher Guide's Digital Answer Key tab.
🩺 Medicine Group

Nurses and doctors calculate medicine doses, patient measurements, and lab results every day. A misplaced decimal point can be dangerous, so precision matters.

1. A nurse gives a patient three doses of medicine: \(12.5\) mL, \(8.75\) mL, and \(6.4\) mL over one day. What is the total amount of medicine given?

2. A patient's temperature was \(100.45\)°F this morning and \(98.6\)°F this afternoon. How many degrees did it drop?

3. \(0.25 \times 40\)

4. \(0.5 \times 36.4\)

5. \(1.5 \times 8\)

6. \(0.75 \times 6\)

7. \(2.2 \times 3.5\)

8. \(3.2 \times 2.5\)

💰 Market Group

Store owners, cashiers, and vendors calculate prices, discounts, and change every day. Being off by even a few cents adds up fast across many customers.

1. A vendor sells apples for \(\$2.35\) per kilogram. A customer buys \(3.4\) kilograms. How much does the customer pay?

2. A cash register has \(\$112.75\) in it at the start of the day. After paying \(\$45.60\) in supplier bills, how much is left?

3. \(1.25 \times 8\)

4. \(3.40 \times 2.5\)

5. \(0.75 \times 12\)

6. \(4.20 \times 3\)

7. \(2.60 \times 5.5\)

8. \(6.5 \times 1.4\)

⚙️ Engineering Group

Engineers measure and build parts that must fit together exactly. A part that is off by even a few hundredths of a centimeter can make a whole machine fail.

1. An engineer joins three metal beams: \(245.6\) cm, \(89.75\) cm, and then removes a \(34.2\) cm section. What is the final length?

2. A part must measure exactly \(12.50\) cm. The measured part is \(12.53\) cm. By how many cm does it differ from the target?

3. \(4.5 \times 3\)

4. \(2.25 + 1.8\)

5. \(0.6 \times 12\)

6. \(15.4 - 3.75\)

7. \(8.2 \times 0.5\)

8. \(3.6 \times 2.5\)

🎨 Arts Group

Artists and designers measure canvases, mix paint ratios, and time performances. Precise measurements keep proportions and timing looking and sounding right.

1. A painter mixes \(2.5\) liters of blue paint, \(1.75\) liters of white paint, and \(0.6\) liters of yellow paint. How many liters of paint does she have in total?

2. A song is \(24.5\) seconds long. A musician repeats a \(1.25\)-second riff \(2\) extra times at the end. How long is the song now?

3. \(3.5 \times 4\)

4. \(0.75 \times 8\)

5. \(12.25 - 4.6\)

6. \(2.4 \times 6.5\)

7. \(5.5 \times 2.2\)

8. \(1.8 \times 0.5\)
