Lesson 1.2 - Add, Subtract, and Multiply Decimals. This is the first content week after Welcome Week (Q01W01). Every day reuses the same standard-algorithm foundation: line up decimal points for addition/subtraction, count total decimal places for multiplication, and always estimate by rounding before calculating exactly.

Weekly Objectives (from the Math 6 Lesson Plan)
  • Explain the place-value reasoning behind aligning decimal points for addition/subtraction versus counting total decimal digits for multiplication.
  • Calculate multi-digit decimal sums, differences, and products using standard algorithms.
  • Use rounding strategies to estimate answers and confirm calculated results.
  • Interpret contextual word problems to determine which operation is required.
  • Evaluate solutions by rounding to find approximate values and confirm problem resolutions.

Challenge Bank: One Level Harder

Extra problems for early finishers or homework extension. Each is a step above that day's practice - more decimal places, chained multi-step reasoning, or (where CCSS.6.NS.B.3 also calls for it) a first light touch of decimal division. Click a problem to reveal its full solution.

Monday - Decimal Operations, Harder Tier

C1. Compute \(124.5 \times 0.036\).

Show solution

Multiply as whole numbers: \(1245 \times 36 = 44{,}820\). Total decimal places: \(1 + 3 = 4\). Shift 4 places left: \(44820 \rightarrow \mathbf{4.482}\).

C2. A lab tech mixes three solutions: \(12.075\) L, \(8.4\) L, and \(0.925\) L. She splits the total evenly into 4 containers. How many liters go in each container?

Show solution

Total \(= 12.075 + 8.4 + 0.925 = 21.4\) L. Divide evenly: \(21.4 \div 4 = \mathbf{5.35}\) L per container.

C3. Find the missing digits \(A\) and \(B\): \(A7.B \times 4 = 110.8\).

Show solution

\(A7.B = 110.8 \div 4 = 27.7\). So \(A = \mathbf{2}\) and \(B = \mathbf{7}\).

Tuesday - Multi-Step Word Problems, Harder Tier

C1. A caterer buys \(15.5\) lbs of chicken at \(\$3.40\)/lb and \(8.25\) lbs of beef at \(\$5.60\)/lb, then pays a \(\$12.50\) delivery fee. She splits the total cost evenly across 4 event budgets. How much does each budget pay?

Show solution

Chicken \(= 15.5 \times 3.40 = \$52.70\). Beef \(= 8.25 \times 5.60 = \$46.20\). Subtotal \(= 52.70+46.20=\$98.90\). Plus delivery: \(98.90+12.50=\$111.40\). Split 4 ways: \(111.40 \div 4 = \mathbf{\$27.85}\) per budget.

C2. A runner's 4 lap times are \(68.45\)s, \(71.2\)s, \(69.85\)s, and \(70.5\)s. What was her average lap time?

Show solution

Total \(= 68.45+71.2+69.85+70.5=280.0\)s. Average \(= 280.0 \div 4 = \mathbf{70.0}\)s per lap.

C3. A store buys 24 phone cases at \(\$4.25\) each and sells them at \(\$9.99\) each. What is the total profit?

Show solution

Cost \(= 24 \times 4.25 = \$102.00\). Revenue \(= 24 \times 9.99 = \$239.76\). Profit \(= 239.76-102.00 = \mathbf{\$137.76}\).

Wednesday - Error Analysis & Health Science, Harder Tier

C1. A student computed \(23.4 - 5.68 + 1.2\) and got \(30.28\). Find the mistake and the correct answer.

Show solution

Mistake: the student added \(5.68\) instead of subtracting it. Correct: \(23.4-5.68=17.72\); \(17.72+1.2=\mathbf{18.92}\).

C2. A student eats \(1.5\) bars of Snack A (\(5.2\) g fat/bar) and \(2\) bars of Snack B (\(3.75\) g fat/bar). What is the total fat?

Show solution

Snack A: \(1.5 \times 5.2 = 7.8\) g. Snack B: \(2 \times 3.75 = 7.5\) g. Total \(= 7.8+7.5 = \mathbf{15.3}\) g.

C3. A student computed \(0.25 \times 0.4\) and got \(1.0\). Find the mistake and the correct answer.

Show solution

Mistake: \(0.25\) has 2 decimal places and \(0.4\) has 1, so the product needs 3 decimal places - the student only shifted 1. Correct: \(25 \times 4 = 100\), shift 3 places: \(\mathbf{0.100 = 0.1}\).

Thursday - Speed & Estimation, Harder Tier

C1. Compute \(0.045 \times 3.2\).

Show solution

\(45 \times 32 = 1440\). Total decimal places: \(3+1=4\). Shift: \(\mathbf{0.144}\).

C2. A cyclist rides \(42.5\) km in \(1.25\) hours. What is her average speed in km/h?

Show solution

\(42.5 \div 1.25 = \mathbf{34}\) km/h.

C3. Compute \(0.008 \times 1.5\).

Show solution

\(8 \times 15 = 120\). Total decimal places: \(3+1=4\). Shift: \(\mathbf{0.012}\).

Friday - Enrichment for Early Finishers

These are original problems, unrelated to the graded assessment, safe to hand out to students who finish the paper test early.

C1. A biologist records 3 measurements: \(45.125\) mm, \(38.75\) mm, and \(52.6\) mm. Find the range (largest minus smallest).

Show solution

\(52.6 - 45.125 = \mathbf{7.475}\) mm.

C2. Compute \(212.5 \times 0.048\).

Show solution

\(2125 \times 48 = 102{,}000\). Total decimal places: \(1+3=4\). Shift: \(\mathbf{10.2}\).

C3. A charity raised \(\$145.60\) on day 1, then \(1.5\) times that amount on day 2. What was the two-day total?

Show solution

Day 2 \(= 145.60 \times 1.5 = \$218.40\). Total \(= 145.60+218.40 = \mathbf{\$364.00}\).

Plan Alignment Notes

Three places where the built student pages and the written weekly plan don't perfectly line up. None of these are broken - they're judgment calls I made that you may want to confirm or override.

1. Monday's Weekly Practice Set doesn't fit in 45 minutes

The printed worksheet (now digitized as Monday Tab 5) adds up to roughly 10-15 more minutes than the 45-minute period has room for once Tabs 1-4 and 6 are taught. I've treated Tab 5 as homework or independent/early-finisher work in the pacing table. If you'd rather it happen in class, something else on Monday needs to be trimmed or moved to a different day.

2. Thursday's Speed Sprint is Station 3, not the opening warm-up

The written plan calls the "Speed & Precision Sprint" the day's warm-up (i.e., first activity), but the built page has Race Splits as Station 1 and Speed Sprint as Station 3. If you want the page to match the plan's stated order, the two station tabs can be swapped - let me know and I'll make that change.

3. Wednesday's Health Science tab isn't named in the plan's activity list

The plan's Real-life Application section for Wednesday references a health/nutrition scenario, but the plan's step-by-step Activities list only mentions Error Analysis, IXL, and the small-group teacher table - it doesn't explicitly schedule time for a separate Health Science activity. I gave it 20 minutes in the pacing table since the content is already built and directly supports the stated real-life application, but confirm that's the intended use of time versus, say, more IXL time.

Also worth knowing: the source Plan PDF's own header is inconsistent

The uploaded weekly plan document labels this week "Q01W01 - Welcome Week, August 17-21, 2025" inside its own text, even though the folder, the lesson content, and the actual calendar (Aug 17, 2026 is a Monday) all point to this being Q01W02 in the 2026-2027 school year, and the content is clearly Lesson 1.2 rather than a welcome/orientation week.
