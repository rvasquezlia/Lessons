Station 2

Sports Analytics: Race Splits

In a 400-meter relay, four runners recorded the following split times in seconds:

  • Runner 1: 12.45 seconds
  • Runner 2: 11.82 seconds
  • Runner 3: 12.09 seconds
  • Runner 4: 11.54 seconds

Task: Calculate the total relay time. Then find the difference between the fastest and slowest split times.

Station 3

Environmental Science: River Gauges

What to do at this station Find the Day 2 height first (Day 1 plus the rise), then subtract the Day 3 drop from that. Type the final reading and click Check My Work.

A scientist is monitoring the height of a river gauge over three days.

  • Day 1: the gauge reads 4.56 meters.
  • Day 2: the water level rises by 0.875 meters.
  • Day 3: the water level drops by 1.2 meters from the Day 2 height.

Task: what is the final gauge reading on Day 3? Show your alignment of decimal points.

Station 4

Peer Review & Defense

What to do at this station Discuss with your group why \(3.78\) looks wrong before doing any exact math - write that reasoning in the first box. Then calculate the real answer, type it in the second box, and click Check My Work.

Problem: a student calculated \(15.75 \times 2.4\) and got \(\mathbf{3.78}\).

Task: without doing the exact math first, use rounding to explain why this answer is not reasonable. Then solve for the correct exact answer.
