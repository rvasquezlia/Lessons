Word Problems: Mixed & Real-World

For the teacher Every problem is a word problem - matches the Mixed Word Problems and Real-World Applications tabs on the on-screen Word Problems page exactly. Print and hand out to early finishers or as homework. Answer key is in the Teacher's Guide's Word Problems answer key tab.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Mixed Word Problems

Read carefully - decide which operation each one needs.

1. A book costs \(\$9.49\). You buy \(2\) copies. What is the total cost?

2. You have \(\$15.00\) and spend \(\$6.75\). How much do you have left?

3. A ribbon is \(3.5\) m long. You cut off \(1.25\) m. How much ribbon remains?

4. Pencils cost \(\$0.65\) each. What is the cost of \(4\) pencils?

5. A jug holds \(2.8\) L. If \(0.6\) L is poured out, how much remains?

6. A snack costs \(\$2.25\). You pay with a \(\$5.00\) bill. How much change do you get?

7. \(3\) notebooks at \(\$1.80\) each plus a \(\$0.75\) eraser. What is the total?

8. A runner covers \(5.2\) km, then \(3.75\) km more. What is the total distance?

9. \(\$40.00\) is split evenly between \(5\) friends. How much does each friend get?

10. A rope is \(12.4\) m long. Three pieces of \(2.5\) m each are cut off. How much rope is left?

11. A cereal box costs \(\$4.29\). A coupon takes \(\$0.50\) off. What is the new price?

12. Stickers cost \(\$0.35\) each. What is the cost of \(6\) stickers?

13. A recipe needs \(1.5\) cups of flour per batch. How many cups for \(4\) batches?

14. The temperature starts at \(68.5^\circ\text{F}\) and drops \(12.3^\circ\text{F}\). What is the new temperature?

15. A \(\$25.00\) gift card is used to buy an item for \(\$18.60\). What balance is left on the card?

Real-World Applications

Apply decimal operations to real-world situations.

1. Gas costs \(\$3.45\) per gallon. What is the cost of \(8\) gallons?

2. A hiker walks \(4.2\) km, rests, then walks \(3.65\) km more. What is the total distance?

3. A recipe needs \(2.25\) cups of sugar per batch. How much sugar for \(3\) batches?

4. A \(\$60.00\) budget covers \(\$24.75\) on food and \(\$18.30\) on supplies. How much money is left?

5. A board is \(8.5\) ft long. Pieces of \(3.25\) ft and \(2.4\) ft are cut off. How much board remains?

6. A phone bill is \(\$45.99\). A \(\$5.00\) credit is applied. What is the new bill?

7. \(5\) friends split a \(\$32.50\) pizza bill evenly. How much does each friend pay?

8. A car travels \(55.5\) miles, then \(48.25\) more miles. What is the total distance?

9. Fabric costs \(\$6.40\) per yard. What is the cost of \(3.5\) yards?

10. A paycheck of \(\$215.60\) has \(\$42.75\) deducted for taxes. What is the take-home pay?

11. A pool holds \(450.5\) gallons. \(62.25\) gallons drain out. How many gallons are left?

12. \(4\) friends buy tickets at \(\$11.25\) each. What is the total cost?

13. A trail is \(9.8\) miles. You have hiked \(6.35\) miles. How many miles remain?

14. A grocery bill has items costing \(\$14.29\), \(\$8.75\), and \(\$3.40\). What is the total?

15. A savings account has \(\$120.50\). A deposit of \(\$35.25\) is made. What is the new balance?
