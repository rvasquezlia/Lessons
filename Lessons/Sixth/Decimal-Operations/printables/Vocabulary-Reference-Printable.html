Word Bank

Decimal Point (\(\cdot\)): Separates whole quantities (left) from fractional parts (right).

Place Value: The value a digit has because of where it sits in a number.

Tenths (\(0.1\)): \(\frac{1}{10}\)th of a whole (1 dime = \$0.10).

Hundredths (\(0.01\)): \(\frac{1}{100}\)th of a whole (1 penny = \$0.01).

Thousandths (\(0.001\)): \(\frac{1}{1000}\)th of a whole (metric labs, sports timing).

Standard Algorithm: The step-by-step written method that lines digits up in columns.

Regroup (Carry): When a column's sum is \(10\) or more, carry \(1\) to the next column left.

Regroup (Borrow): Take \(1\) from the column to the left when subtraction would go negative.

Partial Product: One piece of a multiplication problem before the pieces are added together.

Decimal Places: The number of digits written to the right of the decimal point.

Estimate: An approximate answer found by rounding, used as a reasonableness check.

Round: Replace a number with a nearby, simpler value based on the digit right after the target place.

Sum / Difference / Product: The answers to addition, subtraction, and multiplication problems.

Reasonableness: Whether an exact answer makes sense compared to the estimate.

Keyword Clues for Word Problems

Addition (+): total, sum, combined, altogether, increased by.

Subtraction (−): difference, how much more, change, remaining, decreased by, less than (turns the order around!).

Multiplication (×): product, total of equal groups, area, "times as many," twice, of (in context of groups).

The "Less Than" Turn-Around: "6.5 less than 10.75" means \(10.75 - 6.5\), not \(6.5 - 10.75\) - always write the second number first.
