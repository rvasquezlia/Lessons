Full Review: Like Denominators
Rule Reminder

Same denominator: add or subtract only the numerators, keep the denominator, then simplify.

Worked Example 1: Adding (\(\frac{3}{10} + \frac{4}{10}\))

Full Review: Unlike Denominators & Mixed Numbers
Rule Reminder

Unlike denominators: Butterfly Method - cross-multiply for the wings, multiply the denominators for the new one. Mixed numbers: combine whole numbers and fraction parts separately, regrouping when needed.

Worked Example 1: Unlike Denominators (\(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{6}\))

Worked Example 2: Mixed Number Subtraction With Borrowing (\(3\frac{1}{5} - 1\frac{3}{5}\))

Critical Thinking

Why must fractions have the same denominator before you can add or subtract them, but that's not required before you multiply them?
