Operations with Fractions: Practice Set

How this page works
  • Computation only - no word problems (those are on the Word Problems page).
  • Work the tabs in any order - each groups problems by type and difficulty.
  • Type answers as a fraction like 3/4 or a mixed number like 2 1/3, always in simplest form. Click Check - 2 tries before the method is shown.

Mixed Numbers Practice
What to do on this tab
  • Combine whole numbers and fraction parts separately.
  • Tier B: watch for a fraction sum that's improper - regroup it into the whole number.
  • Tier C: watch for a fraction being subtracted that's bigger than what you have - regroup a whole first.
