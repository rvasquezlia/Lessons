Operations with Fractions: Word Problems

How this page works
  • Every problem is a word problem - decide whether it's asking you to add or subtract, watching for keywords like "total," "combined," "difference," or "how much is left."
  • Work the tabs in any order: estimate with a benchmark fraction first, then calculate the exact answer, then type it in and check.
  • Type answers as a fraction like 3/4 or a mixed number like 2 1/3, always in simplest form.
