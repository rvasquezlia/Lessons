Real-World Decimal Jigsaw: Group Cards

For the teacher Print one section per table (cut apart or photocopy separately) and hand each table only their theme's card - 6 real-world problems each, no bare computation drills. Students solve on this card, then type and check their own answers on the class page (which matches exactly). After finishing, tables get ~15 minutes to mix and visit another group. Students scan or print-to-PDF their completed card as part of their exit ticket. Answer key is in the Teacher Guide's Digital Answer Key tab.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Medicine Group

Nurses and doctors calculate medicine doses, patient measurements, and lab results every day. A misplaced decimal point can be dangerous, so precision matters.

Medicine 1. A nurse gives a patient three doses of medicine: \(12.5\) mL, \(8.75\) mL, and \(6.4\) mL over one day. What is the total amount of medicine given?

Medicine 2. A patient's temperature was \(100.45\)°F this morning and \(98.6\)°F this afternoon. How many degrees did it drop?

Medicine 3. A pharmacy technician has a bottle with \(50\) mL of solution. She dispenses three prescriptions of \(12.5\) mL, \(9.75\) mL, and \(14.25\) mL. How much solution remains in the bottle?

Medicine 4. A patient must take \(2.5\) mL of medicine every 6 hours, 4 times a day. How much medicine total does the patient take in one day?

Medicine 5. A hospital orders 3 boxes of syringes at \(\$18.75\) each, plus a box of bandages for \(\$9.40\). What is the total cost of the order?

Medicine 6. A lab technician needs \(0.75\) mL of a solution for each of 12 test tubes. How much solution does she need in total?

Market Group

Store owners, cashiers, and vendors calculate prices, discounts, and change every day. Being off by even a few cents adds up fast across many customers.

Market 1. A vendor sells apples for \(\$2.35\) per kilogram. A customer buys \(3.4\) kilograms. How much does the customer pay?

Market 2. A cash register has \(\$112.75\) in it at the start of the day. After paying \(\$45.60\) in supplier bills, how much is left?

Market 3. A vendor buys 6 crates of oranges at \(\$8.25\) each, then sells all of them for a total of \(\$72.90\). What is her profit?

Market 4. A cashier gives change from a \(\$20\) bill for a purchase of \(\$13.45\). How much change is given?

Market 5. A store marks down a \(\$45.80\) jacket by \(\$12.25\). What is the new price?

Market 6. A shopper buys \(2.5\) kg of coffee beans at \(\$9.60\) per kg. How much does she pay?

Engineering Group

Engineers measure and build parts that must fit together exactly. A part that is off by even a few hundredths of a centimeter can make a whole machine fail.

Engineering 1. An engineer joins three metal beams: \(245.6\) cm, \(89.75\) cm, and then removes a \(34.2\) cm section. What is the final length?

Engineering 2. A part must measure exactly \(12.50\) cm. The measured part is \(12.53\) cm. By how many cm does it differ from the target?

Engineering 3. A machinist welds two metal sheets measuring \(3.75\) mm and \(2.6\) mm thick together, then grinds off \(0.85\) mm from the total. What is the final thickness?

Engineering 4. An engineer needs 8 bolts that each weigh \(0.45\) kg. What is the total weight of the bolts?

Engineering 5. A support beam measuring \(4.2\) m is extended by welding on a \(1.85\) m section. What is the new total length?

Engineering 6. A gear part must be \(6.75\) cm in diameter. The manufactured part measures \(6.82\) cm. By how many cm is it oversized?

Arts Group

Artists and designers measure canvases, mix paint ratios, and time performances. Precise measurements keep proportions and timing looking and sounding right.

Arts 1. A painter mixes \(2.5\) liters of blue paint, \(1.75\) liters of white paint, and \(0.6\) liters of yellow paint. How many liters of paint does she have in total?

Arts 2. A song is \(24.5\) seconds long. A musician repeats a \(1.25\)-second riff \(2\) extra times at the end. How long is the song now?

Arts 3. A sculptor mixes \(1.5\) kg of clay with \(0.75\) kg of a second clay and \(0.35\) kg of a hardening additive. What is the total weight of the mixture?

Arts 4. A dance routine has 4 sections that each last \(2.25\) minutes. How long is the whole routine?

Arts 5. An art teacher buys 6 canvases at \(\$4.85\) each. What is the total cost?

Arts 6. A photographer's video clip is \(45.6\) seconds long. She trims \(8.75\) seconds off the start and \(6.4\) seconds off the end. How long is the clip now?
