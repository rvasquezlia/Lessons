Keyword Analysis: these clue words help you decide which operation a word problem needs.

  • Addition (+): total, sum, combined, altogether, increased by.
  • Subtraction (-): difference, how much more, change, remaining, decreased by.
  • Multiplication (×): product, total of equal groups, area, "times as many."
