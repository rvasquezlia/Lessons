Topic: Decimal Operations & Place Value Alignment

  • Decimal Point (\(\cdot\)): Separates Whole Numbers from Parts of a Whole.
  • Tenths (\(0.1\)): \(\frac{1}{10}\text{th}\) of a whole (1 dime = $0.10).
  • Hundredths (\(0.01\)): \(\frac{1}{100}\text{th}\) of a whole (1 penny = $0.01).
  • Thousandths (\(0.001\)): \(\frac{1}{1000}\text{th}\) of a whole (Metric lab scales & sports split-times).
  • Standard Algorithm: A reliable, step-by-step procedure for computing sums, differences, or products.
  • Estimation / Rounding: Replacing exact values with nearby round numbers to check reasonableness.

Estimation Strategy: How and Why We Round

Before calculating exact answers, we round numbers to estimate the result. Estimation acts as a safety check against place-value errors!

The Rounding Rule:
  1. Identify your target place value position.
  2. Look at the digit directly to its right.
  3. If that digit is 5 or greater, round up. If it's 4 or less, keep the target digit the same.

Real example: Round 14.86 to the nearest tenth. The hundredths digit is 6. Since \(6 \ge 5\), \(14.86 \rightarrow 14.9\).
