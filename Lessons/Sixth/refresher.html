Operation Keywords

Word problems hide clues that tell you which operation to use.

Copy Into Math Notebook
  • Addition (+): sum, total, altogether, combined, in all, increased by, perimeter.
  • Subtraction (−): difference, how many more, how much left, remaining, decreased by, less than, fewer.
  • Multiplication (×): product, times, each (finding a total), in all (equal groups), area, of.
  • Division (÷): quotient, split equally, shared equally, divided into, per, out of.

Try It

1. A library had 4,520 books. After a donation, the total was increased by 850 books. How many books are there altogether?

2. A theater has 24 rows of seats with 15 seats in each row. How many seats are there in all?

1. Addition: \(4{,}520 + 850 = 5{,}370\) books.
2. Multiplication: \(24 \times 15 = 360\) seats.
Multi-Digit Whole Number Word Problems
Copy Into Math Notebook
  1. Read the problem and underline the key action words.
  2. Align place-value columns strictly (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands).
  3. Regroup or borrow carefully across multi-zero numbers.
  4. For long division: Divide → Multiply → Subtract → Bring Down.

Try It: Addition & Subtraction

1. School A collected 12,450 cans. School B collected 14,880 cans. What is the combined total collected by both schools?

2. A stadium has 45,000 seats. For a concert, 38,240 tickets were sold. How many seats were left empty?

1. 27,330 cans (\(12{,}450 + 14{,}880\))
2. 6,760 empty seats (\(45{,}000 - 38{,}240\))

Try It: Multiplication & Long Division

1. A delivery truck makes 32 trips carrying 425 kilograms per trip. What is the total payload weight transported?

2. A factory produces 2,880 toys over a 24-hour shift. How many toys are manufactured per hour?

1. 13,600 kg (\(425 \times 32\))
2. 120 toys per hour (\(2{,}880 \div 24\))
Decimal Operations Word Problems
Copy Into Math Notebook

Decimal alignment: in money, distance, and measurement problems, line up the decimal point before calculating. Fill missing place values with zeros.

Multiplication: count the total decimal digits in both factors and place the decimal point that many places from the right in the product. Division by a whole number: place the decimal point in the quotient directly above the decimal point in the dividend.

Try It: Addition & Subtraction

1. A worker cuts 4.25 meters and 2.8 meters from a 10.0-meter timber beam. What length of timber remains?

2. John ran 5.6 kilometers on Saturday and 8.45 kilometers on Sunday. What is his total distance combined?

1. 2.95 meters remaining (\(10.00 - 4.25 - 2.80 = 2.95\))
2. 14.05 kilometers total (\(5.60 + 8.45 = 14.05\))

Try It: Multiplication & Division

1. Gasoline costs $3.25 per gallon. If a driver buys 12 gallons, what is the total cost?

2. A bill of $48.60 is divided equally among 4 friends. How much does each person pay?

1. $39.00 (\(3.25 \times 12 = 39.00\))
2. $12.15 per person (\(48.60 \div 4 = 12.15\))
Operations With Fractions
Copy Into Math Notebook
  • Adding/subtracting: convert to a common denominator first, then add or subtract the numerators.
  • Multiplying: multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.
  • Dividing: multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction (“keep, change, flip”).

Try It: Addition & Subtraction

1. A student completed \(\frac{3}{4}\) of his project on Friday and \(\frac{1}{8}\) on Saturday. What fraction of the project has he finished altogether?

2. A container held \(\frac{7}{10}\) liters of milk. A chef used \(\frac{2}{5}\) liters for a sauce. How much milk is left in the container?

1. \(\frac{7}{8}\) of the project (\(\frac{6}{8} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{7}{8}\))
2. \(\frac{3}{10}\) liters remaining (\(\frac{7}{10} - \frac{4}{10} = \frac{3}{10}\))

Try It: Multiplication & Division

1. A farm covers 20 acres. Crops are planted on \(\frac{3}{4}\) of the farm. How many acres are planted with crops?

2. A chef has 4 kilograms of rice. Each serving requires \(\frac{1}{3}\) kilogram. How many total servings can be made?

1. 15 acres (\(20 \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{60}{4} = 15\))
2. 12 servings (\(4 \div \frac{1}{3} = 4 \times 3 = 12\))
Geometric Measurement & Classification
Copy Into Math Notebook

Perimeter (P): the total distance around the outside edge of a 2D shape — add all side lengths together.

3D figures: faces (flat surfaces), edges (where two faces meet), vertices (corner points). Prisms have 2 identical parallel bases; pyramids have 1 base and triangular faces meeting at a point.

Try It: Perimeter

1. A square playground has a side length of 15.5 meters. What is its total perimeter?

2. A triangle has sides measuring 8 cm, 11 cm, and 14 cm. Calculate its perimeter.

1. 62 meters (\(15.5 \times 4 = 62\))
2. 33 cm (\(8 + 11 + 14 = 33\))

Try It: 3D Figure Classification

Identify the 3D figure from each description:

1. A figure with 6 identical square faces, 12 edges, and 8 vertices.

2. A figure with 1 square base and 4 triangular side faces meeting at a point.

1. Cube (a square prism).
2. Square pyramid.

An athletic team orders 4 water bottles at $2.75 each and 2 towels at $5.50 each. The tax added is $1.20. What is the total cost of the order?
