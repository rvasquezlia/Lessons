A student account never sees this page or the links above - only an account listed as a teacher can get past the sign-in screen here. Use the tabs below to find what you need.

Signing In
Sign in with your school Google account - the same one you already use for email. There's no separate password to remember and nothing to set up first. Signing in here, on the Lessons Index, or on either dashboard all use the same sign-in.
What You'll See as a Teacher

Once you're signed in as a teacher, the Lessons Index shows every grade and every topic, not just one class - and you'll see the three links at the top of this page and both dashboards on every teacher-only page, so you can always get back to any of them.

Being listed as someone's teacher on the class list is not, by itself, what unlocks this. A separate, short list of teacher accounts controls it - see Managing Your Class List below.

If Your Dashboard Looks Empty

This almost always means your name is spelled slightly differently in two places on the spreadsheet - once next to your own account, and once next to each of your students. Whoever manages the class list can fix this by making sure both match exactly, or by leaving your own entry blank so you see every student.

Grades & Tracks

Most students are on a plain grade - 6, 7, or 8. Two tracks run alongside a regular grade rather than replacing it:

  • 7th Grade Honors - uses the exact same Rational Numbers unit as regular 7th grade, plus one extra unit of its own, Squares, Cubes & Roots.
  • 8th Grade Pre-AP - has its own unit, Linear Functions, and its own STREAM project, Ethical Linear Budgeting.
Spelling Matters Here

If you're entering a student's grade on the class list, an Honors or Pre-AP student needs to be typed exactly 7-Honors or 8-PreAP - not "7 Honors" or "Honors 7." A typo here is the most common reason a student can't see their own unit.

Getting Around the Dashboard

The dashboard opens on a Home screen with a search box and three tiles - pick one to get to work. The filters at the top (Grade, Activity, Teacher, and a "Flagged only" checkbox) apply everywhere, including Home's own tiles. A "← Teacher Dashboard Home" link at the top of every other screen brings you back, with quick-switch buttons next to it so you can jump straight to any of the three without going all the way back to Home first.

Search for a student

Type a name into Home's search box and every matching student shows up as you type - click the one you want. It searches your whole class list, even if today's Grade filter doesn't include them.

1. My Class

Leads with what's worth a look first: open flags, students who are stalled or haven't started anything, and your lowest-scoring activities and students. Below that sits a compact summary (average score, Effort Score), and tucked under "More charts & detail" is the fuller picture: progress by unit, and a chart of when students are actually doing the work during the day. Two links take you to the full breakdown by unit or by activity - opening a page's own details there also shows the most common wrong answer on each question, so you can see exactly what's confusing students.

2. Students

One row per student, including anyone who hasn't started anything yet. Click a name for their full profile, which is also where you'll find their Effort Score, how much of their own coursework they've completed, and how often they get something right on a second try after missing it the first time.

3. Flags to Review

Opens straight to Flags & Behavior - anything worth a second look (see the table below). Two supporting views sit alongside it: Engagement Funnel shows who's opening work but not finishing it, and Full Submission Log is the complete, raw record everything else on the dashboard is summarized from.

What triggers a flag?

Everything below is noticed after the fact, from the timing already saved when a student works - nothing is watched live while a student is working.

FlagWhat it means
Fast-guessingThe first answer came in under 3 seconds.
Attempt-1 sacrificeA suspiciously fast wrong first try, then a slow, correct second try.
Reflection padding / low-effort reflectionA written answer that's repetitive filler, or too short to be meaningful.
Idle gapA pause of 3 to 15 minutes between actions in the same sitting.
Tab-skippingTwo tabs opened less than 2 seconds apart - clicking through without reading.
Paste / right-click / lost focusDetected on an answer field. Losing focus is only flagged after 3 or more times.
Possible shared answersTwo different students gave the same wrong answer on the same question within 15 minutes.
Possible answer lookupA paste, a very high score, and a completion time under half the class average.
On a paired or team STREAM project, teammates' answers are expected to match - they're working on the same shared attempt by design (see STREAM Projects below). The dashboard already knows this and skips "Possible shared answers" between known teammates.

Giving a student their attempts back

How to reset

Open a student's activity from Students, By Activity, or the Submission Log, then use the "Give attempts back" toolbar:

  1. Choose a scope: one question, a whole section, or the entire activity.
  2. Click the matching Reset button. This takes effect the next time the student opens the page - nothing changes on their screen right away.
  3. Nothing is ever deleted - a reset adds a new entry to the record, so the full history stays visible and their score starts fresh from that point.

Marking a flag reviewed

Once you've looked into a flag, mark it Reviewed and choose Valid concern (something you followed up on) or Not an issue (a false alarm, like two teammates or a genuinely fast learner). This doesn't remove the flag from the record - it just lets you and any colleague see at a glance which flags still need attention.

Driver & Navigator

Every team has one Driver (types the answers, moves the pieces, submits) and one or more Navigators (can look around and follow along, but can't answer or submit anything themselves). A Navigator sees the Driver's saved progress the next time they open the page, not instantly as the Driver works.

Setting Up a Pair or a Team
On the class list
  1. Open the Pairs tab in the shared spreadsheet.
  2. For a pair of two: add one row for each student - their own email, their partner's email, the project, and their own role (Driver or Navigator).
  3. For a team of three or more: add one row per student, all sharing one short team label for that project - one Driver, the rest Navigators.

To undo a pairing - the wrong student was added, or a partner needs replacing - open that student's row on the Teacher Dashboard and click Unpair. It takes effect the next time that student opens the page.

Getting a Student's Real Certificate, Poster, or Garden

From either dashboard's project view, open a student's entry in the Deliverables list and click "Open & download real files." This opens that student's own project page with their saved work already loaded in, read-only, showing the exact same download buttons they have. What you get is their genuine, full-quality certificate, badge, garden, sign, or poster - never a copy, a screenshot, or a lower-quality stand-in. Nothing you do on that page is saved over the student's real work.

To collect every student's certificate for a project at once, click "Download all certificates" next to that project's Deliverables list. It opens one browser tab per student, one at a time, saves that student's real certificate, then closes itself before moving to the next - you'll get a short summary when it's done, including anyone skipped because they haven't finished the second day yet. This covers the certificate only; badges, stamps, gardens, signs, and posters still need "Open & download real files," one student at a time. If your browser asks to allow pop-ups the first time, allow them and click the button again - it's safe to run more than once.

Frequently Asked Questions
A student says they can't see an activity.
Check three things in order: their grade on the class list matches the activity's own grade exactly (including the Honors/Pre-AP spelling), that activity is turned on, and their email on the class list is spelled exactly like their school Google account.
I don't see any students on my dashboard.
This is almost always a small spelling mismatch between your own name on the Teachers tab and how it's spelled next to your students - see Getting Started above.
A student wants to redo an activity they already finished.
Use "Give attempts back" from their detail view on the Teacher Dashboard - never edit the spreadsheet directly to undo their work.
How do I know if a flag is worth following up on?
Open the student's detail view and look at the Full Submission Log for the exact timing or answer that triggered it, then mark it Reviewed as Valid concern or Not an issue so you or a colleague don't have to look into the same flag twice.
Where do I find a unit's answer key?
Sign in as a teacher directly on that lesson page - every question fills in with the correct answer automatically and a banner appears confirming you're viewing it as a teacher. There's no separate answer key document.
