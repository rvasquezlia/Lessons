🗺️ Site Map - Where Everything Lives

The whole site is four kinds of page, all reachable from the nav buttons at the top of this page and every dashboard:

Lessons Index (index.html)

What students see first. Signing in shows only their own grade's units; a teacher signing in here instead sees every grade and every unit unrestricted, plus these three nav buttons to reach the dashboards and this help page.

Lesson pages (per unit)

The actual student work - Vocabulary, Explanation, Practice, Word Problems, Test Prep, Review, and a Teacher's Guide for every unit. See The 7 Page Types below.

Teacher Dashboard

The main gradebook and behavior monitor - every student, every activity, every submission, with tools to flag, reset, and review. See below.

Projects Dashboard

A separate, zoomed-out view of only the hands-on STREAM projects (Eco-Garden, Ethical Auditor, Youth Festival, Ethical Linear Budgeting) - see below.

Both dashboards and this help page are teacher-only - a student account signing in anywhere on this site never sees them and has no link to find them. Access is controlled by the Teachers tab in the shared Sheet (see Managing the Roster), never by hiding the URL.

📚 The 7 Page Types in Every Unit

Every unit (Decimal Operations, Integers, Linear Equations, ...) ships the same set of page types, so once you know how one unit reads, you know how all of them read:

Two units (Operations with Rationals and Literal Equations) also have an eighth page, Guided Solving Ladder - a step-by-step scaffolded solving drill. It's gated and graded like the others, but deliberately isn't linked from the Lessons Index - find it from that unit's own Teacher's Guide.

🎓 Grades & Tracks

Most students are on a plain numeric grade (6, 7, or 8). Two tracks sit alongside a regular grade instead of replacing it:

  • 7th Grade Honors (7-Honors) - shares every Rational Numbers page verbatim with regular 7th grade, plus its own extra unit, Squares, Cubes & Roots.
  • 8th Grade Pre-AP (8-PreAP) - its own unit, Linear Functions, and the newest STREAM project, Ethical Linear Budgeting.
A student's Grade cell in the Roster must match one of these five values exactly (spelling and hyphenation) - 7-Honors, not 7 Honors or Honors 7.

🔒 Sign-In & Access

Everyone signs in with their school Google account (@lincoln.edu.ni). There are no separate passwords or accounts to manage - access is entirely controlled by two Sheet tabs:

A student

Must have a row on Roster with their email, grade, and teacher. They then only see units matching their own grade, and can only work on activities listed as Active on ActivityCatalog.

A teacher

Must have a row on Teachers - being listed as someone's teacher on Roster is not enough by itself. Being on Teachers unlocks the Lessons Index unrestricted, both dashboards, this help page, and an automatic answer key on every lesson page.

If a teacher's dashboard shows no students at all, the most common cause is a typo in the optional Scope column on Teachers - it must exactly match that teacher's own name as spelled on Roster, or it silently shows nobody instead of erroring. Leave Scope blank (or write All) to see every student.

📊 Teacher Dashboard - Reading the Four Tabs

Filters at the top (Grade, Activity, Teacher, and a "Flagged only" checkbox) apply across all four tabs at once. Every tab follows a list, then click for detail pattern - click any row to drill in, and a "Back" button returns you to the list.

1. Overview

A bird's-eye summary: stat tiles, a progress-by-unit bar chart, your lowest-scoring activities and students, a count of every flag by category, and a 24-hour histogram of when students are actually doing the work.

2. Unit & Lesson Deep Dive

By Unit groups whole units together; By Activity breaks it down page-by-page, including activities nobody has started yet. Opening an activity's detail view also shows Item Diagnostics - the most common wrong answer per question, so you can see exactly which misconception is spreading.

3. Student Roster & Profiles

One row per student - including students who haven't started anything yet. Columns include an Effort Score Index (0-100, blending depth of engagement, reflection quality, and flag-free work), a Lesson Completion %, and an Attempt-2 Recovery Index (of everything they missed on the first try, what share did they eventually get right?).

4. Integrity & Behavior Monitor

Three sub-tabs: Engagement Funnel (Not started / Opened only / In progress / Completed) tells you who's opening work but not finishing it; Flags & Behavior lists every integrity/effort signal (see below) plus a time-vs-score scatter plot; Full Submission Log is the raw, complete event feed everything else on the dashboard is summarized from.

What triggers a flag?

All flags are read after the fact from timestamps already saved - nothing is monitored live. The main ones:

FlagWhat it means
Fast-guessingFirst attempt answered in under 3 seconds.
Attempt-1 sacrificeA suspiciously fast wrong first try, then a slow, correct second try.
Reflection padding / low-effort reflectionA written answer that's either repetitive filler or too short to be meaningful.
Idle gapA pause of 3-15 minutes between actions in the same sitting.
Tab-skippingTwo tabs opened less than 2 seconds apart - clicking through without reading.
Paste / right-click / tab-focus lossDetected on an answer field. Focus loss is only flagged at 3+ occurrences.
Possible shared answersTwo different students gave the identical wrong answer on the same item within 15 minutes.
Possible answer lookupA paste event, a very high score, and a completion time under half the class average.
On a paired/team STREAM project, teammates' answers are mirrored to each other by design (see STREAM Projects & Pairing below) - so "Possible shared answers" between two known teammates is expected, not suspicious, and the dashboard now skips it automatically for anyone paired together.

Giving a student their attempts back

Open a student's activity detail view (from Student Roster, By Activity, or the Submission Log) to find the "Give attempts back" toolbar:

  1. Pick a scope: reset one item, an entire section/tab, or the whole activity.
  2. Click the matching Reset button. This takes effect the next time the student loads the page - nothing changes live.
  3. A reset never deletes anything - it appends a new "reset" entry, so the full history stays visible, and attempt numbering (and scoring) restarts cleanly from there.

Marking a flag reviewed

Any flagged row can be marked Reviewed once you've looked into it, and classified as either a Valid concern (something you followed up on) or Not an issue (a false alarm - e.g. two teammates, or a legitimately fast learner). This doesn't remove the flag from the record, but it lets you tell at a glance which flags still need a look.

🧩 Projects Dashboard

A separate page focused only on the hands-on STREAM projects, since a single number on the main dashboard can't show "did this class actually touch Science this unit?" the way this page can.

  • STREAM pillar rings - six rings (Science, Technology, Religion, Engineering, Art, Math), aggregated across every project currently in view. A pillar with no matching content anywhere shows "No data yet" rather than a fake 0%.
  • Project cards - one per project, with its own mini pillar breakdown, an attempts-per-item stat (how many tries it typically takes the class to land the right answer - handy for spotting a station that's too hard), and a collapsible Deliverables table summarizing what each student actually built (their budget total, garden layout, chosen materials, etc.).
The Deliverables table is a text summary reconstructed from saved data - it is not a picture of the student's actual canvas/poster. Nothing rendered on a student's canvas is ever uploaded anywhere; the certificate/badge/poster they download stays on their own device only.

Driver & Navigator

Every team has exactly one Driver (types the answers, drags the pieces, submits) and one or more Navigators (read-only - sees the Driver's saved progress on their own screen, can look around, can never edit or submit). Mirroring happens on the Driver's next save, not instantly as they type.

Setting up a pair or a team of three or more

  1. Open the Pairs tab in the shared Sheet.
  2. For a pair: add one row per student - each student's own email, the other student's email under PartnerEmail, the activity's ID, and that student's own Role (Driver or Navigator). Leave TeamId blank.
  3. For a team of 3+: add one row per student, all sharing the exact same TeamId (any short label, e.g. T1) for that activity - one Driver, the rest Navigators. Leave PartnerEmail blank on these rows.

To undo a pairing (wrong student added, or a partner needs replacing), open that student's row on the Teacher Dashboard's Student Roster or Submission Log detail view and click Unpair - it takes effect the next time that student loads the page.

📋 Managing the Roster & Sheet

Everything a teacher needs to hand-edit lives in one Google Sheet. You never need to touch code to add a student, add a class, or change who's a teacher.

TabYou edit this to...
RosterAdd/remove a student, or fix their grade or assigned teacher.
ActivityCatalogTurn an activity on/off (Active column) or check which grade it belongs to.
TeachersGrant/revoke dashboard access, or scope a teacher to only their own students.
PairsSet up or remove a paired/team STREAM project assignment.
ProgressAutomatic only - never hand-edit except the two "reviewed" columns, which the dashboard now writes for you.
Any change to the underlying grading/backend code (not the Sheet data itself) needs a manual redeploy in the Apps Script editor before it takes effect - that step is always called out explicitly whenever it applies.

❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

A student says they can't see an activity.
Check three things in order: (1) their Roster.Grade matches the activity's ActivityCatalog.Grade exactly (including Honors/Pre-AP spelling), (2) that activity's Active column is checked, (3) their email on Roster is spelled exactly as their Google account's own email.
I don't see any students on my dashboard.
Almost always a Teachers.Scope typo - it has to match your name on Roster.Teacher exactly, or it silently shows nobody. Leave it blank to see everyone.
A student wants to redo an activity they already finished.
Use "Give attempts back" from their detail view on the Teacher Dashboard (see above) - never edit the Sheet directly.
How do I know if a flag is something worth following up on?
Open the student's detail view, check the Full Submission Log for the exact timing/answer that triggered it, then mark it Reviewed as Valid or Not an issue so you (or a colleague) don't have to re-investigate the same flag later.
Can I see the actual picture of a student's poster/garden/certificate?
Not from the dashboard today - only a text summary of their choices. The rendered image is generated in the student's own browser and downloaded straight to their device; it's never uploaded anywhere.
Where do I find a unit's answer key?
Sign in as a teacher directly on that lesson page - every problem auto-fills with the correct answer and a "TEACHER VIEW" banner appears. No separate answer key document exists.
