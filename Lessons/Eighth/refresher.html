Translating Words Into Algebra

Setting up an equation is usually the hardest part — these clue words tell you exactly what symbol to write.

Copy Into Math Notebook
SymbolClue WordsExample
= (equals)is, was, equals, results in, gives“the total is 92” → = 92
+ (add)sum, more than, increased by, plus, added to“5 more than x” → x + 5
− (subtract)difference, decreased by, minus, less than, fewer than“5 less than x” → x − 5 (NOT 5 − x)
× (multiply)product, times, twice, triple, of (with %)“twice a number” → 2x
÷ (divide)quotient, per, divided equally, split into equal parts“split equally among 3” → x ÷ 3

Warning: “less than” reverses order! “5 less than a number” → \(x - 5\) (NOT \(5 - x\)). Test it: if a number is 5 less than 20, the answer is 15, so it must be \(x - 5\).

Try It

1. “7 more than a number”

2. “A number decreased by 12 is 5”

3. “The product of 4 and a number is 36”

4. “8 less than a number” (watch the word order!)

1. \(x + 7\)
2. \(x - 12 = 5 \rightarrow x = 17\)
3. \(4x = 36 \rightarrow x = 9\)
4. \(x - 8\) (not \(8 - x\); “less than” flips the order)
PEMDAS: Order of Operations
Copy Into Math Notebook

Parentheses → Exponents → Multiplication & Division (left to right) → Addition & Subtraction (left to right).

Try It

1. Evaluate \(5 + 2 \times (8 - 3)^2\)

2. Evaluate \((12 - 4) \div 2 + 3^2\)

1. 55 (\(5 + 2 \times 25 = 5 + 50\))
2. 13 (\(8 \div 2 + 9 = 4 + 9\))
Integer Operations
Copy Into Math Notebook

Add/subtract: same signs → add the absolute values and keep the sign; different signs → subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value. Subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite.

Multiply/divide: same signs → positive result; different signs → negative result.

Try It

1. Evaluate \(-14 + 9\)

2. Evaluate \(7 - (-12)\)

3. Evaluate \((-7) \times 8\)

4. Evaluate \((-45) \div (-5)\)

1. −5 (\(14 - 9 = 5\), keep the negative sign)
2. 19 (\(7 + 12\))
3. −56
4. 9 (same signs, positive result)
Evaluating Algebraic Expressions
Copy Into Math Notebook

Substitute the given value(s) for the variable(s), then apply the order of operations.

Try It

1. Evaluate \(2a + 7\) when \(a = -3\).

2. Evaluate \(x^2 - 4x + 1\) when \(x = 5\).

1. 1 (\(2(-3) + 7 = -6 + 7\))
2. 6 (\(25 - 20 + 1\))
Distributive Property & Combining Like Terms
Copy Into Math Notebook

Distribute first: \(a(b + c) = ab + ac\). Then combine like terms (same variable and exponent) by adding or subtracting their coefficients.

Try It

1. Simplify \(4(x - 3) + 2x\)

2. Simplify \(-2(3y + 1) + 5y\)

1. \(6x - 12\) (\(4x - 12 + 2x\))
2. \(-y - 2\) (\(-6y - 2 + 5y\))
One-Step & Two-Step Equations
Copy Into Math Notebook

Use the inverse operation to isolate the variable. Whatever you do to one side of the equation, you must do to the other. For two-step equations: undo addition/subtraction first, then undo multiplication/division.

Try It: One-Step

1. Solve \(x - 8 = 13\)

2. Solve \(\dfrac{x}{4} = -9\)

1. \(x = 21\)
2. \(x = -36\)

Try It: Two-Step

1. Solve \(2x - 5 = 11\)

2. Solve \(-4x + 7 = -9\)

1. \(x = 8\) (\(2x = 16\))
2. \(x = 4\) (\(-4x = -16\))
Coordinate Plane Review
Copy Into Math Notebook

Points are written as ordered pairs \((x, y)\). Quadrant signs: I \((+,+)\), II \((-,+)\), III \((-,-)\), IV \((+,-)\).

Try It

1. Which quadrant is the point \((4, -6)\) in?

2. State the directions to plot \((-3, -5)\) starting from the origin.

1. Quadrant IV (x positive, y negative)
2. Move 3 units left, then 5 units down.
