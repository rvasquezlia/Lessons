Error Detective

For the teacher Print and hand out one per student or pair. Students find the first wrong step, explain the mistake, and solve correctly, then type their findings on the class page. Answer key is in the Teacher Guide's Printables & Key tab.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.
