Equation Classification Challenge

For the teacher Print and hand out to small groups. Groups predict each equation's classification (one solution, no solution, or infinitely many), then verify algebraically before typing their answers on the class page. Answer key is in the Teacher Guide's Printables & Key tab.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Predict, Then Verify

For each equation: predict whether it has one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions. Then solve to verify your prediction.

1. \(2x + 5 = 3x - 1\)

2. \(2(x + 3) = 2x + 7\)

3. \(4(x + 1) = 4x + 4\)

4. \(6x - 2 = 3(2x + 1)\)

5. \(5(x - 2) = 5x - 10\)

6. \(7x + 3 = 4x + 18\)

7. \(8x - 4 = 8x + 3\)

8. \(3(x - 2) = 3x - 6\)
