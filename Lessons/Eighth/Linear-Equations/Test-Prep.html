Part 1: Integer Operation Laws (Sign Rules)
OperationThe Rule
AdditionSame Signs: Add the absolute values and keep the sign. (\(-3+(-5)=-8\))
Different Signs: Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger; keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value. (\(-10+4=-6\))
SubtractionAdd the Opposite: Change the subtraction sign to addition and change the sign of the second number, then follow addition rules. (\(5-(-2)\) becomes \(5+2=7\))
Multiplication & DivisionSame Signs: Always Positive. Different Signs: Always Negative.
The Golden Rule of Equations: whatever you do to one side of the equation, you MUST do to the other side.
Part 2: Inverse Operations
If the equation shows...InverseAction to take on BOTH sides
Addition (\(+5\))↔Subtract 5
Subtraction (\(-8\))↔Add 8
Multiplication (\(3x\))↔Divide by 3
Division (\(\frac{x}{4}\))↔Multiply by 4

Remember the Classification Rules

Simplify each side fully. A false statement (\(6=7\)) means no solution. A true statement (\(4=4\)) means infinitely many solutions. Anything else gives exactly one solution.
