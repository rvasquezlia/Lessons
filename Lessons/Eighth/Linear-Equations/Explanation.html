Foundations: Properties of Equality & Inverse Operations
Integer Rules You'll Need
OperationThe Rule
AdditionSame signs: add, keep sign. Different signs: subtract, keep the larger's sign.
SubtractionAdd the opposite: \(5-(-2) \rightarrow 5+2=7\).
Multiply/DivideSame signs → positive. Different signs → negative.
The Golden Rule: an equation is a balanced scale. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.
Properties of Equality
  • If \(a=b\), then \(a+c=b+c\).
  • If \(a=b\), then \(a-c=b-c\).
  • If \(a=b\), then \(ac=bc\).
  • If \(a=b\) and \(c\neq0\), then \(\frac{a}{c}=\frac{b}{c}\).

When the Variable Disappears
  • \(2(x+3)=2x+7 \rightarrow 6=7\) - false → no solution.
  • \(4(x+1)=4x+4 \rightarrow 4=4\) - true → infinitely many solutions.
Classifying Solutions: One, None, or Infinitely Many?
Simplify both sides. False (\(6=7\)) → no solution. True (\(4=4\)) → infinitely many. Anything else → one solution.

Worked Applications: Writing Equations From Words
The 4-Step Method
  1. Define: choose a letter, state what it represents.
  2. Write the equation.
  3. Solve with inverse operations.
  4. Interpret as a sentence with units.
Example 1: The Taxi Ride

Flat fee \$5.00 plus \$2.00/mile. Total cost \$17.00 - how many miles?

1. Define: \(m\) = miles.

2-3. Equation & Solve: \[2m + 5 = 17 \rightarrow m = 6\]

4. Interpret: The ride was 6 miles.

Example 2: Mixture

Prepare 12 L of 30% acid by mixing 50% and 20% solutions.

1. Define: \(x\) = liters of 50%; \((12-x)\) = liters of 20%.

2-3. Equation & Solve: \[0.5x + 0.2(12-x) = 3.6 \rightarrow x = 4\]

4. Interpret: 4 L of 50% solution, 8 L of 20% solution.

Example 3: Work & Time

Printer A: 5 pages/min. Printer B: 8 pages/min. Together, 260 pages - how long?

1. Define: \(x\) = minutes.

2-3. Equation & Solve: \[5x + 8x = 260 \rightarrow x = 20\]

4. Interpret: 20 minutes together.
