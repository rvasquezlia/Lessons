Strategy Challenge: Group Cards

For the teacher Print one section per table (cut apart or photocopy separately) and hand each table only their group's card. Students solve on this card, then pick the matching group letter on the class page (which locks in) and type their solution and strategy explanation. Answer key is in the Teacher Guide's Printables & Key tab.
