Lesson 1-2: Mathematical Literacy and Vocabulary

Example. What is the solution of the equation \(8x - 4(x-6) + 8 = 44\)? Justify and explain your work.

StepsWorkJustification
First, write the equation.\(8x-4(x-6)+8=44\)Original equation
Second, distribute the \(-4\).\(8x-4x+24+8=44\)Distributive Property
Then, combine like terms.\(4x+32=44\)Simplify.
Next, subtract 32 from both sides.\(4x=12\)Subtraction Property of Equality
Finally, divide both sides by 4.\(x=3\)Division Property of Equality

Lesson 1-2: Reteach to Build Understanding

An equation is a mathematical sentence with an equal sign. If a sentence is true for a value of the variable in the equation, that value is called a solution of the equation. For \(x+2=8\), the solution is 6, because when 6 is substituted in the equation for \(x\), the equation is true: \(6+2=8\).

1. Match each lettered step for solving \(x+3(2x-1)=11\) with its justification: Distributive Property, Combine like terms, Addition Property of Equality, Division Property of Equality.

a. \(x+6x-3=11\)   b. \(7x-3=11\)   c. \(7x-3+3=11+3\)   d. \(\dfrac{7x}{7}=\dfrac{14}{7}\)   (\(x=2\))

2. Describe and correct the error Cameron made when solving \(6x-2(x-5)=-2\): \(6x-2x-10=-2 \rightarrow 4x-10=-2 \rightarrow 4x-10+10=-2+10 \rightarrow \dfrac{4x}{4}=\dfrac{8}{4} \rightarrow x=2\).

3. Complete the missing expressions and equations to show the solution of \(7x-10+4x=34\).
