Section 4: Applications36 points

4 questions · 9 points each · write an equation, solve it, and answer in a full sentence

15. Phone Plan A costs \(\$30\) per month plus \(\$0.10\) per text message. Phone Plan B costs a flat \(\$45\) per month with unlimited texting. At how many text messages, \(x\), do both plans cost the same?

16. The sum of two consecutive odd integers is \(76\). Write and solve an equation to find both integers.

17. A tank contains \(40\) liters of water. Water is added at a rate of \(5\) liters per minute. After how many minutes, \(x\), will the tank contain \(100\) liters?

18. A furniture store offers two rental plans for a couch. Plan A costs \(\$25\) down plus \(\$15\) per month. Plan B costs \(\$85\) total with no monthly fee. After how many months, \(x\), does Plan A cost the same as Plan B?
