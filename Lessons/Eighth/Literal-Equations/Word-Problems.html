Attacking a Word Problem in Three Moves
MoveWhat you do
1. Name itWhich formula fits this scenario, and which letter is the unknown?
2. Climb the ladderVariable? Obstruction? Operation? Inverse?
3. SubstitutePlug in the given numbers and compute the final answer.
Worked Example: A rectangular garden has an area of \(96\) sq ft and a length of \(12\) ft. Find its width. One Step
Move 1: Name It
Which formula fits?\(A = lw\)
Which letter is unknown?\(w\)
Move 2: Climb the Ladder
What is obstructing \(w\)?\(l\), multiplying
What's the inverse operation?Division
\(w = \dfrac{A}{l}\)
Move 3: Substitute
Plug in \(A = 96\), \(l = 12\)\(w = \dfrac{96}{12}\)
\(w = 8\) ft

Finance: Simple Interest
\(I = Prt\) and \(A = P + Prt\) show up constantly in banking and loans - being able to solve either one for any letter is a real financial-literacy skill. \(A = P + Prt\) needs the factoring question first, since \(P\) appears twice.

Comparing Two Investments

Investment A: \(\$500\) at \(4\%\) simple interest for \(6\) years. Investment B: \(\$600\) at \(3\%\) simple interest for \(6\) years.

Task: find the interest earned by each investment, then say which one earns more and by how much.
