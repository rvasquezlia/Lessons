Literal Equations: Mixed Practice / Assessment

For the teacher This mirrors the Test Prep page's Mixed Practice tab and can double as a paper assessment - keep this link and the answer key (Teacher's Guide) confidential from students. 10 scored questions (100 points) plus one extra-credit problem. Print one copy per student.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Name: _______________________ Grade and Section: _______________ Date: _______________ Score: _____ / 100
Instructions

Solve each formula for the given variable, or solve each scenario, showing every step - full credit requires visible work, not just a final answer.

  • You have 45 minutes to complete this assessment.
  • Calculators are permitted - this is Eighth Grade, where calculators are allowed all year.
  • For Section 1, write only the expression for the requested variable (for example, \(A/w\)), with parentheses around any multi-term numerator or denominator.
  • The Extra Credit problem at the end is optional and will not lower your score if left blank.
Section 1: Bare Rearrangements20 points

4 questions · 5 points each · solve for the given variable

1. \(V=lwh\), solve for \(w\).

2. \(y=mx+b\), solve for \(m\).

3. \(2a+3b=18\), solve for \(b\).

4. \(A=\frac12bh\), solve for \(h\).

Section 2: Error Analysis20 points

2 questions · 10 points each · find the correct rearrangement

5. \(P=2l+2w\), solve for \(w\). A student wrote \(w=P-2l\). What is the correct rearrangement, and what did the student forget?

6. \(A=P+Prt\), solve for \(r\). A student wrote \(r=\dfrac{A}{Pt}\), forgetting to subtract \(P\) first. What is the correct rearrangement?

Section 3: Word Problems60 points

4 questions · 15 points each · identify the formula, solve for the needed variable, then substitute

7. A rectangular room has an area of \(180\) square feet and a length of \(15\) feet. What is its width?

8. Convert \(25\)°C to Fahrenheit.

9. A train travels at \(75\) mph for \(3.5\) hours. How far does it travel?

10. An account earns \(\$96\) in interest on a \(\$600\) principal at a rate of \(4\%\). How many years was it invested?

Extra Credit

11. A cylindrical can's total surface area is \(S=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh\). If \(S=471\) cm\(^2\), \(r=5\) cm, and \(\pi\approx3.14\), find \(h\).
