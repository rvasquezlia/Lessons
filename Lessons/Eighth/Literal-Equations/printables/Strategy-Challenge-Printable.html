Word Problem Station Cards

For the teacher Print one section per table for a three-station rotation (Geometry, Science, Finance). Each station's problems all use the same formula family, so groups practice recognizing which letter to solve for before rearranging. Answer key is in the Teacher's Guide.

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Station 1: Geometry

1. A rectangular pool has an area of \(150\) square feet and a width of \(10\) feet. Find its length.

2. A circular garden has a circumference of \(62.8\) feet. Using \(C=2\pi r\) and \(\pi\approx3.14\), find its radius.

3. A cylindrical can has a volume of \(235.5\) cubic centimeters and a radius of \(5\) centimeters. Using \(V=\pi r^2h\) and \(\pi\approx3.14\), find its height.

Station 2: Science

1. Convert \(15\)°C to Fahrenheit.

2. A jet flies \(1{,}500\) miles in \(2.5\) hours. Find its average speed.

3. Sound travels \(686\) meters in \(2\) seconds in a certain experiment. Find the speed of sound in this scenario.

Station 3: Finance

1. A \(\$1{,}500\) principal earns \(\$270\) in interest over \(6\) years. Find the interest rate.

2. A \(\$2{,}000\) principal grows to a total of \(\$2{,}500\) after \(5\) years of simple interest. Find the interest rate.

3. An account earns \(\$84\) in interest at a rate of \(3.5\%\) over \(4\) years. Find the principal.
