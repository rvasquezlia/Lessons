The Three-Step Check

1. Undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division. 2. If your target letter appears more than once, factor it out before dividing. 3. Check your work by substituting numbers into both the original formula and your rearranged one - they should agree.

Practice: Solve for the Given Variable

1. \(C=2\pi r\), solve for \(r\).

2. \(V=\pi r^2h\), solve for \(h\).

3. \(d=rt\), solve for \(r\).

4. \(I=Prt\), solve for \(t\).

5. \(P=2l+2w\), solve for \(l\).

6. \(y=mx+b\), solve for \(x\).

7. \(L=a+(n-1)d\), solve for \(d\).

8. \(A=P+Prt\), solve for \(P\).
