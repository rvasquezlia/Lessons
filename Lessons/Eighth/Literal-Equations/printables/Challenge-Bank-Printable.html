Challenge Bank

For the teacher Print and hand out to early finishers or as homework, any time during this topic. No solutions are shown here (they stay in the Teacher's Guide's Challenge Bank tab).

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Explanation-Tier Challenges

C1. Ohm's Law: \(V = IR\). Solve for \(R\).

C2. Solve \(2(x+y) = z\) for \(y\).

C3. The average of two numbers is \(m = \dfrac{a+b}{2}\). Solve for \(b\).

Practice-Set-Tier Challenges

C1. Solve \(3x+2y=12\) for \(y\).

C2. Kinetic energy: \(KE = \frac12mv^2\). Solve for \(m\).

C3. Solve \(ax-b=cx+d\) for \(x\) (the target variable is on both sides).

Word-Problems-Tier Challenges

C1. The average of three test scores is \(m=\dfrac{a+b+c}{3}\). Two scores are \(82\) and \(91\), and the average is \(88\). What was the third score?

C2. Ohm's Law: \(V=IR\). A circuit has a voltage of \(240\) V and a resistance of \(60\) Ω. What is the current?

C3. A ball's velocity while decelerating under gravity follows \(v=v_0-gt\), with \(g=9.8\) m/s\(^2\). If the ball's velocity is \(0\) m/s after \(2\) seconds, what was its initial velocity \(v_0\)?

Test-Prep Enrichment for Early Finishers

C1. Solve \(5x+3y=2x-y+20\) for \(y\).

C2. The thin-lens formula is \(\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b=\dfrac1f\). Solve for \(a\).

C3. Two pipes filling a tank together follow \(\dfrac{x}{a}+\dfrac{x}{b}=1\), where \(x\) is the time to fill it together. Solve for \(x\).
