Slope & Rate of Change
The Slope Formula

To find the slope \(m\) between two points \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\): \(m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\). A horizontal line has slope 0. A vertical line has an undefined slope.

Writing Equations in Slope-Intercept Form
Slope-Intercept Form

\(y = mx + b\), where \(m\) is the slope and \(b\) is the y-intercept. Find \(m\) first, then substitute one point to solve for \(b\).

Function Notation: Evaluating Functions
Evaluating a Function

\(f(a)\) means: substitute \(a\) for \(x\) in the function's rule, then simplify.
