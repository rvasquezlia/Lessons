Privacy Policy

Lincoln International Academy Lessons & Progress site

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Lincoln International Academy Lessons & Progress site collects, how it is used, and who can see it. This site is provided by Lincoln International Academy for its own students and teachers to complete classroom lessons and track academic progress.

Who can use this site

Access is limited to people with a Lincoln International Academy Google account. Sign-in uses Google's own "Sign in with Google" service, restricted to the school's Google domain. There are no separate passwords or accounts to create, and this site never sees or stores a password.

Information we collect

  • Account information - the name and email address on file with your Lincoln International Academy Google account, provided to us by Google when you sign in.
  • Academic activity - answers you submit, scores, completion status, and the general timing of your work on a lesson, associated with your signed-in account so your own teacher can review it.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not collect any information beyond what is needed to run a classroom lesson activity and show a student's own progress to their teacher.

How we use this information

  • To confirm you are a current student or teacher at Lincoln International Academy before showing any lesson content.
  • To save your progress on a lesson so you can pick up where you left off.
  • To let your teacher review your work, provide feedback, and understand how the class as a whole is doing.

Who can see your information

A student's academic activity is visible only to that student's own teachers at Lincoln International Academy - never to other students, and never to anyone outside the school. We do not sell or share this information with outside companies for advertising or any other purpose.

The site is built using standard Google Workspace services (Google Sign-In and Google's own cloud tools) to operate securely; these services process information only on the school's behalf, to make the site work, not for their own purposes.

How long we keep information

Academic records are kept for as long as the school needs them for normal recordkeeping. A parent, guardian, or teacher may contact the school to ask about or request removal of a specific student's record.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for use by enrolled Lincoln International Academy students under the supervision of their teachers and school, using a school-issued account. It is not open to the general public. A parent or guardian with questions about their child's information should contact the school directly.

Security

Access is limited to signed-in members of the school community, and every request is independently re-checked against the school's own records - we do not rely on information a browser claims about itself. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely without risk, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change to reflect it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about a specific student's information should be directed to Lincoln International Academy directly.
