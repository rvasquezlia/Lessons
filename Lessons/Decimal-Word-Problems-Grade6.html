Title: Decimal Word Problem Vocabulary

  • Add when two or more amounts are being combined into a total.
  • Subtract when you're finding change, how much more/less, or what's left over.
  • Multiply when the same price or amount happens each time, for several items or people.
  • Decimal Tip: Always line up the decimal points before you add or subtract. Add extra zeros so both numbers have the same number of decimal places.
