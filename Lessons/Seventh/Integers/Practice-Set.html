How this page works Pure computation - no word problems here, those are on the Word Problems page. Work through the 5 tabs in any order your teacher assigns. Copy each problem into your notebook, solve it there first, then type just your final answer and check it. You get up to 2 tries per problem - after a second miss, the correct answer is shown and it locks. When you finish, type your name above and click Print My Progress. Calculators are not allowed this quarter.
Opposites & Absolute Value
What to do on this tab Find the opposite, the absolute value, or compare using \(<, >,\) or \(=\) - as asked.
Add Integers
What to do on this tab Same signs: add and keep the sign. Different signs: subtract and keep the sign of the larger absolute value.
Subtract Integers
What to do on this tab Use Keep-Change-Change to rewrite every subtraction as addition before solving.
Multiply & Divide Integers
What to do on this tab Same signs give a positive result; different signs give a negative result - for both multiplication and division.
Mixed Computation & Order of Operations
What to do on this tab First identify which operation you're doing, then apply the correct sign rule. For problems with parentheses or exponents, work the innermost parentheses (and exponents) first, then finish left to right.
