Number Vocabulary: Integers, Opposites & Absolute Value
What to do on this tab Read and copy each term into your notebook. Then try the 3 quick checks at the bottom - you get 2 tries per question before the answer is shown.
Whole Numbers

The numbers used for counting, starting from zero: \(0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots\). They don't include fractions, decimals, or negatives.

Integers

All the whole numbers, their negative counterparts, and zero: \(\ldots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots\). Every whole number is an integer, but not every integer is a whole number.

Positive Numbers

Any number greater than zero - to the right of zero on a number line.

Negative Numbers

Any number less than zero - to the left of zero on a number line.

Opposite Integers (Additive Inverse)

Two numbers the same distance from zero but in opposite directions. \(5\) and \(-5\) are opposites. The opposite of \(0\) is \(0\). "Additive inverse" is another name for the same idea - you'll see it again in the subtraction rule.

Absolute Value \(|x|\)

A number's distance from zero, always non-negative. \(|5| = 5\) and \(|-5| = 5\) - direction doesn't matter, only distance.

Visualizing Opposites: \(-4\) and \(4\) - both are exactly 4 units from zero, just in opposite directions.

Operation Vocabulary: Naming the Parts and the Strategies
What to do on this tab These are the words your teacher and your textbook use for the parts of each operation, plus the two strategy names you'll rely on for the rest of this topic. Copy them into your notebook, then try the matching check.
Addend & Sum

An addend is a number being added. The sum is the result of addition. In \(-6 + 5 = -1\), \(-6\) and \(5\) are addends and \(-1\) is the sum.

Difference

The result of subtraction. In \(8 - 15 = -7\), \(-7\) is the difference.

Factor & Product

A factor is a number being multiplied. The product is the result of multiplication. In \(-4 \times 5 = -20\), \(-4\) and \(5\) are factors and \(-20\) is the product.

Quotient

The result of division. In \(-20 \div 4 = -5\), \(-5\) is the quotient.

Same Signs / Different Signs

Shorthand for whether two numbers are both positive, both negative (same signs), or one of each (different signs) - the very first thing you check before adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing integers.

Keep-Change-Change

The name of the strategy for subtracting integers: keep the first number, change subtraction to addition, change the sign of the second number. It rewrites every subtraction as an addition problem you already know how to solve.
