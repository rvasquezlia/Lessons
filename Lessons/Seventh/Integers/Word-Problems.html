Addition & Subtraction Word Problems
Clue Words Reference

Positive (+): Deposit, Gain, Above Sea Level, Ascend, Credit, Rise.
Negative (-): Withdraw, Loss, Below Sea Level, Descend, Debt, Debit.

Guided Example: Temperature

Scenario: It was \(-8\)°F and rose \(12\)°F.

Expression: \[-8 + 12 = 4\]

Result: The new temperature is \(4\)°F.

Bank Balance (multi-step)

Malena's account starts at \(-\$85\) (she overdrew it). She deposits \(\$40\), then deposits \(\$25\), then withdraws \(\$30\).

Task: find her final balance.

Temperature Change (multi-step)

The temperature at dawn was \(-4^\circ\text{C}\). By noon it rose \(11^\circ\text{C}\). By evening it had dropped \(15^\circ\text{C}\) from its noon value.

Task: find the noon temperature and the evening temperature.

Multiplication & Division Word Problems
Clue Words Reference

Multiplication Clues: Rate, Per, Each, Every.    Division Clues: Shared, Average, Split, Per.

Guided Example: Stock Market

Scenario: A stock drops \$3 each day for 4 days.

Expression: \[-3 \times 4 = -12\]

Result: The total change is \(-\$12\).

Stock Market (multi-step)

A stock's value changed by the same amount each day for \(6\) trading days, for a total change of \(-\$42\).

Task: find the average daily change. Then, if it continues at that same daily rate for \(3\) more days, find the additional change.

Electric Charge

A balloon gains \(8\) extra electrons after being rubbed on hair. Each electron carries a charge of \(-1\) (in elementary charge units).

Task: find the total charge added to the balloon.
