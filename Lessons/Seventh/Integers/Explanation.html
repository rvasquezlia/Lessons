Relating Integers to Their Opposites; Absolute Value
The Rules
  • Opposite: flip the sign.
  • Absolute value: drop the sign - always 0 or positive.
  • Compare: farther right on the number line = greater, regardless of absolute value.

Adding Integers
The Rule
  • Same signs: add the absolute values, keep the common sign.
  • Different signs: subtract the smaller from the larger, keep the sign of the larger.
\(-3 + 5 = 2\): from 0, move left 3, then right 5.

Subtracting Integers
Keep-Change-Change
  • Keep the first number.
  • Change subtraction to addition.
  • Change the second number's sign.
\(-4 - 9 = -13\): arrow starts at \(-4\), moves 9 more left.
