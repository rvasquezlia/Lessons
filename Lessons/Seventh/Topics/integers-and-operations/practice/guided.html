Lesson 1.4: Multiply and Divide Integers
Same signs → positive. Different signs → negative. This rule holds for both multiplication and division - and for division, remember that division "undoes" multiplication, so the sign rule is identical.

Applications: Stock Market & Electric Charge
Multidisciplinary connection: Financial Literacy and Physical Science. Multiplying integers scales a repeated change - a daily stock loss repeated over several days, or a repeated electric charge added by many electrons.

Real-Life Scenario: Stock Market

For this problem Divide the total change by the number of days to find the average daily change, then multiply that daily rate by 3 more days.

A stock's value changed by the same amount each day for \(6\) trading days, for a total change of \(-\$42\).

Task: find the average daily change. Then, if it continues at that same daily rate for \(3\) more days, find the additional change.

Real-Life Scenario: Electric Charge

For this problem Multiply the number of electrons by the charge each one carries.

A balloon gains \(8\) extra electrons after being rubbed on hair. Each electron carries a charge of \(-1\) (in elementary charge units).

Task: find the total charge added to the balloon.
