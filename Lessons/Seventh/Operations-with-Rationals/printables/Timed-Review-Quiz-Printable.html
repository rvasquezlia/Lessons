Set A: Sign Rules & Concepts

2. Select all statements that are TRUE.

Same signs give a positive product or quotient. · A negative number divided by a negative number is negative. · \(-0.6 \times 0.6\) is negative. · Two negative fractions multiplied together give a positive product. · \(-\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{3}{4} = -1\) · \(0.5 \times (-0.5)\) is positive.
