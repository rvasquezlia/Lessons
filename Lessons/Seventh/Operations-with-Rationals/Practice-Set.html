Operations with Rational Numbers - Practice Set

Practice Set: Computing With Signed Fractions & Decimals

How this page works Pure computation here - no word problems, just numbers. Work through the 5 tabs in whatever order your teacher assigns. Solve each problem in your notebook first, then type your final answer and check it. Match the answer format to the problem: a fraction tab wants a fraction (simplest form), a decimal tab wants a decimal, and the Mixed tab takes either. You get 2 tries per problem; miss twice and the correct answer is shown. Calculators are not allowed this quarter.
Add & Subtract Fractions
What to do on this tab Use the butterfly method (cross-multiply) to combine the fractions first, then apply the same-sign/different-sign rule. Enter your answer as a fraction in simplest form (a whole-number result like -1 is fine as-is).
Ask Yourself, Every Time
  • Subtraction? → Keep-Change-Change first.
  • Same denominator? No → Butterfly Method.
  • Same or different signs? Add or subtract the wings; keep the right sign.
Add & Subtract Decimals
What to do on this tab Line up the decimal points, then use the same-sign/different-sign rule.
Ask Yourself, Every Time
  • Subtraction? → Keep-Change-Change first.
  • Same signs → add, keep the sign.
  • Different signs → subtract smaller from larger, keep the sign of the larger.
Multiply & Divide Fractions
What to do on this tab Same signs give a positive result; different signs give a negative result. Use Keep-Change-Flip for division, and simplify to lowest terms. Enter your answer as a fraction (a whole-number result like 2 is fine as-is).
Ask Yourself, Every Time

Multiplying or dividing? (Dividing → Keep-Change-Flip first.) Same signs or different signs? Same → positive. Different → negative.

Multiply & Divide Decimals
What to do on this tab Same signs give a positive result; different signs give a negative result - multiply or divide as usual, then attach the correct sign.
Ask Yourself, Every Time

Same signs or different signs? Same → positive. Different → negative.

Mixed Computation & Order of Operations
What to do on this tab Some of these mix fractions and decimals in the same problem - convert one form to the other before you compute. Work parentheses first, then multiplication/division left to right, then addition/subtraction left to right. Either a fraction or a decimal is accepted here.
Ask Yourself, Every Time

Are both numbers in the same form? If not, convert one first. Then, at each step: same signs or different signs? Answer that question fresh every time, all the way to the final answer.
