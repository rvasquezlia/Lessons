Adding & Subtracting Signed Fractions
The Question Ladder
#Ask...Then...
1Addition or subtraction?Subtraction → Keep-Change-Change first.
2Same denominator?No → Butterfly Method (cross-multiply for wings, multiply denominators for the new one).
3Same signs or different signs?Same → add wing values. Different → subtract smaller from larger.
4What sign, and is it simplified?Keep the sign of the greater absolute value; simplify by the GCF.

Adding & Subtracting Signed Decimals
The Question Ladder
#Ask...Then...
1Addition or subtraction?Subtraction → Keep-Change-Change first.
2Decimal points lined up?Add placeholder zeros so both numbers have the same number of places.
3Same signs or different signs?Same → add and keep the sign. Different → subtract smaller from larger, keep the sign of the larger.

Multiplying & Dividing Rational Numbers
The Question Ladder
#Ask...Then...
1Multiplying or dividing fractions?Dividing → Keep-Change-Flip first (keep, change ÷ to ×, flip the reciprocal).
2Same signs or different signs?Multiply/divide the absolute values either way.
3What sign does the result get?Same → positive. Different → negative.

Switching Between Fractions & Decimals Mid-Problem
The One Extra Question
Ask...Then...
Are both numbers in the same form?No → convert one before combining. Converting never changes the value or sign.
Which way should I convert?To decimal if the fraction is "clean" (\(\frac12,\frac14,\frac34,\frac18,\frac1{10},\frac15\)); to fraction if the decimal repeats.

Once both numbers match, finish with the same Question Ladder from Tabs 1-3.
