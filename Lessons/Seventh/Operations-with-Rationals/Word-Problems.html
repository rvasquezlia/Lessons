Word Problems: Rational Numbers in Real Life

How this page works Every item here is a word problem involving fractions, decimals, or both. Use the clue words to decide the sign and the operation, then compute with the rules you already know - converting forms whenever that makes the arithmetic easier. Addition & Subtraction Word Problems
Clue Words Reference

Positive (+): Deposit, Gain, Rise, Credit, Increase, Profit.
Negative (-): Withdraw, Loss, Drop, Debit, Decrease, Deficit.

How to read the guided example below: the left column is the real math, rewritten checkpoint by checkpoint down to the final answer (green box). The right column is the same short questions from the Explanation page - only the green box is the actual answer.
Guided Example: Temperature Word Problem
\(-0.75 + 2.25\)
How do I translate the words into an expression?
It was \(-\frac{3}{4}\)°F (\(-0.75\)) at dawn; "rose \(2\frac{1}{4}\)" means add \(2.25\).
↓
\(-0.75 + 2.25\)
Same signs or different signs?
Different
What do I do with the numbers?
Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger.
Resolving\(2.25 - 0.75 = 1.5\)
↓
Noon temperature: \(1.5\)°F
Which number has the greater absolute value?
\(2.25\), so the answer is positive.

Bank Balance (multi-step)

Diego's account starts at \(-\$62.50\) (overdrawn). He deposits \(\$45.75\), then deposits half of \(\$30\), then withdraws \(\$10.25\).

Task: find his final balance.

Temperature Change (multi-step)

The temperature at sunrise was \(-1\frac{1}{2}\)°C. By afternoon it rose \(4\frac{3}{4}\)°C. By night it had dropped \(2.25\)°C from its afternoon value.

Task: find the afternoon temperature and the night temperature.

Multiplication & Division Word Problems
Clue Words Reference

Multiplication Clues: Rate, Per, Each, Every.    Division Clues: Shared, Average, Split, Per.

Guided Example: Submarine Descent Word Problem
\(-15.75 \times 4\)
How do I translate the words into an expression?
"Descends" means a negative rate: \(-15.75\) m per minute, for \(4\) minutes.
↓
\(-15.75 \times 4\)
Same signs or different signs?
Different
What do I do with the numbers?
Multiply the absolute values.
Resolving\(15.75 \times 4 = 63.00\)
↓
Total change: \(-63.00\) m
What sign does the product get?
Different signs - negative.

Elevation Rate & Projection (multi-step)

A hiking group's elevation changed by a total of \(-56.25\) m over a \(7.5\)-minute descent, at a constant rate.

Task: find the rate of change per minute. Then, if the group keeps descending at that same rate for \(4\) more minutes, find the additional elevation change.

Recipe Cost

A cook substitutes 6 ingredients for cheaper versions, changing the recipe's total cost by \(-\$0.75\) for each substitution.

Task: find the total change to the recipe's cost.
