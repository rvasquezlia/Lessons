How this page works
  • Pure computation - no word problems. Work the 6 tabs in any order your teacher assigns.
  • Solve in your notebook first, then type your answer and check it - 2 tries before the answer is shown.
  • Fraction answers: type like 3/5 (no spaces, already simplified).
  • Done? Type your name above and click Print My Progress.
Classify & Simplify
What to do on this tab For 1-4, type "yes" or "no". For 5-8, simplify the fraction to lowest terms and type it as, for example, 2/3.
Fractions to Decimals
What to do on this tab Divide to convert each fraction to a decimal. If it repeats, round to the nearest thousandth (3 digits after the decimal point).
Decimals to Fractions
What to do on this tab Convert each decimal to a fraction in simplest form, typed like 3/5.
Compare & Order
What to do on this tab Convert both values to the same form, then type <, >, or =.
Fraction Operations (Review)
What to do on this tab Add, subtract, multiply, or divide as shown. Give your answer as a fraction in simplest form, like 5/12.
Decimal Operations (Review)
What to do on this tab Line up decimal points for add/subtract, and count decimal places for multiply/divide, just like in Explanation.
