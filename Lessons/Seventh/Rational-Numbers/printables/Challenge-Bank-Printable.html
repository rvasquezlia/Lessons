Rational Numbers - Extra Resources

Challenge Bank

For the teacher Print and hand out to early finishers or as homework at any point in this topic. No solutions are shown here (they stay in the Teacher Guide's Printables & Challenge Bank tab).

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Number System & Conversion - Harder Tier

C1. Convert \(\dfrac{7}{11}\) to a decimal. Identify the repeating block.

C2. Order from least to greatest: \(0.7,\ \dfrac{5}{7},\ 0.72,\ \dfrac{13}{18}\).

C3. Which is larger, \(\dfrac{9}{13}\) or \(0.69\)?

C4. Convert \(0.0625\) to a fraction in simplest form.

C5. Is every integer a rational number? Explain using the definition \(\frac{a}{b}\).

Fraction & Decimal Operations - Harder Tier

C1. Evaluate \(\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6} - \dfrac{1}{4}\).

C2. Evaluate \(3.6 \times 0.25\).

C3. Evaluate \(\dfrac{5}{8} \div \dfrac{15}{16}\).

C4. Evaluate \(\left(\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{3}\right) \times 6\).

C5. Evaluate \(4.5 \div 1.25\).

Error Analysis - Harder Tier

C1. A student computed \(\dfrac{3}{4} \div \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{8}\). Find the mistake and the correct answer.

C2. A student computed \(0.7 - 0.35 = 0.45\). Find the mistake and the correct answer.

C3. A student computed \(\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}\). Find the mistake and the correct answer.

C4. A student computed \(2.5 \times 0.1 = 2.5\). Find the mistake and the correct answer.

C5. A student says \(\dfrac{5}{6}\) is bigger than \(\dfrac{7}{8}\) "because 6 is less than 8." Find the mistake and the correct comparison.

Real-World Precision - Harder Tier

C1. A tank holds 12.5 gallons. If \(\dfrac{2}{5}\) of the tank is full, how many gallons are in the tank?

C2. A rope 8.4 m long is cut into pieces 0.6 m each. How many pieces?

C3. A recipe needs \(\dfrac{3}{4}\) cup sugar and yields 6 cookies. How much sugar per cookie?

C4. Find the average of \(0.75\), \(\dfrac{5}{8}\), and \(0.8\).

C5. A car's gas tank is \(\dfrac{3}{8}\) full and holds 16 gallons total. How many more gallons are needed to fill it completely?

Enrichment for Early Finishers

C1. Find a fraction between \(\dfrac{1}{4}\) and \(\dfrac{1}{3}\).

C2. Write three different fractions equivalent to \(\dfrac{2}{5}\).

C3. A decimal repeats in blocks of 2 and equals \(\dfrac{4}{33}\). Find the decimal.

C4. If \(0.1\overline{6} = \dfrac{1}{6}\), what is \(0.8\overline{3}\) as a fraction?

C5. True or False: between any two rational numbers, there is always another rational number. Explain.
