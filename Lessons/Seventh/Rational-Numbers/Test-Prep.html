Concept Review: Every Rule in One Place
What to do on this tab Skim the rules below, then answer the 4 Check Your Understanding problems.
1. Converting Fractions & Decimals
  • Fraction → decimal: divide numerator by denominator. \(\frac34 = 0.75\), \(\frac13 = 0.\overline3\)
  • Decimal → fraction: place value, then simplify. \(0.6 = \frac{6}{10} = \frac35\)
2. Comparing

Convert to the same form, then compare. Farther right = greater.

3. Fraction & Decimal Operations
  • Fractions: common denominator to add/subtract; straight across to multiply; Keep-Change-Flip to divide.
  • Decimals: line up points to add/subtract; count places to multiply; shift points to divide.

Check Your Understanding

Use the rules above to solve these four problems. Fraction answers should be in simplest form, like 5/12.

Common Misconceptions to Watch For
  • Adding fractions straight across instead of finding a common denominator.
  • Misaligning decimal points when adding/subtracting.
  • Miscounting decimal places when multiplying.
  • Dividing fractions without flipping the second one first.
