Quick Check 1.1

1. Which of these situations can be represented by the opposite of \(-25\)?

A. A scuba diver is 25 feet below sea level.
B. Leon gets paid $25.
C. An airplane descends 25 feet.
D. Sara has a $25 debt.

2. A number line represents Allie's scores in the first two rounds of a card game: an arrow from \(0\) to \(4\) shows Round 1, and an arrow from \(4\) back to \(0\) shows Round 2. Select all statements that explain Allie's gains and losses.

She gains 4 points in Round 1. · She gains 4 points in Round 2. · She loses 4 points in Round 1. · She loses 4 points in Round 2. · She has 0 points at the end of two rounds. · She has 4 points at the end of two rounds.

3. Kristen and her family go on a cave tour. They climb down 8 meters below the ground level. To return to the ground level, they climb the opposite of \(-8\) meters. What is the total distance they climbed, counting both directions?

4. A train is going on a journey. First, it travels east for 50 kilometers. Then, it turns north and travels for 22 kilometers. Next, it goes west for 50 kilometers. Finally, it travels south for a certain number of kilometers. At the end of its journey, it is back at its original location. How many kilometers did the train travel south?

5. On Friday, the temperature was \(82^\circ\text{F}\). The temperature changed by \(-2^\circ\text{F}\) on Saturday, and then it changed by \(2^\circ\text{F}\) on Sunday. What was the temperature on Sunday?

Quick Check 1.2

1. Select all the sums that are positive.

\(-5 + (-2)\) · \(5 + (-2)\) · \(-3 + 4\) · \(0 + (-3)\) · \(-2 - (-4)\)

2. David is thinking of two numbers, \(x\) and \(y\). He says that if he adds them together, the sum is negative. Select all the correct explanations of how this is possible.

Both numbers are negative. · \(x\) is negative, and \(|x| > |y|\). · \(x\) is negative, and \(|x| < |y|\). · \(y\) is negative, and \(|x| < |y|\). · \(y\) is negative, and \(|x| > |y|\).

3. Sandra played a matching game. She scored 18 points for correct matches and \(-6\) points for incorrect matches. How many points did Sandra make?

4. When you add a positive number and a negative number, how can you tell whether the sum will be positive or negative?

A. The sum will always be negative.
B. The sum will always be positive.
C. The sum will have the sign of the addend with the lesser absolute value.
D. The sum will have the sign of the addend with the greater absolute value.

5. Ashley's math average decreased 12 points to 78. Then she completed an extra credit assignment and raised her final average by 5 points. How did Ashley's average change from her starting average to her final average?

Quick Check 1.3

1. A number line shows an arrow starting at \(-4\) and ending at \(-13\). Which subtraction expression does it show?

A. \(-13 + 4\)   B. \(-4 - 13\)   C. \(-4 - 9\)   D. \(-4 + 9\)

2. Suppose \(y\) is a positive integer. If \(x - (-y) = z\), will \(z\) be greater than or less than \(x\)? Explain.

A. \(z\) is greater than \(x\), since \(-(-y)\) is the same as subtracting \(y\).
B. \(z\) is greater than \(x\), since subtracting \(-y\) is the same as adding its additive inverse, \(y\).
C. \(z\) is less than \(x\), since subtracting \(-y\) is the same as subtracting its additive inverse, \(y\).
D. \(z\) is less than \(x\), since \(-(-y)\) is the same as adding \(-y\).

3. The temperature in Anchorage, Alaska, was \(-3^\circ\text{C}\) in the afternoon. After the sun went down, the temperature dropped 6 degrees. What was the temperature after the sun went down?

4. Rob borrows $15.00 from his father, and then he borrows $3.00 more. Write an equation using negative integers to represent Rob's debt. How much money does Rob owe his father?

5. Adrienne and Suki simplified the expression \(-8 - (-6)\). Adrienne's work gives \(-8-(-6)=-14\); Suki's work gives \(-8-(-6)=-2\). Whose answer is correct? Explain where the error was made.

A. Suki's; Adrienne subtracted 6 instead of subtracting \(-6\).
B. Suki's; Adrienne subtracted 8 instead of subtracting \(-8\).
C. Adrienne's; Suki added 6 instead of subtracting \(-6\).
D. Adrienne's; Suki added 8 instead of subtracting 8.

Quick Check 1.4

1. Choose whether each product is positive or negative.

\(-3 \cdot (-15)\) · \(x(-x)\) · \(-5 \cdot (-2) \cdot (-1)\) · a negative number times two positive numbers

2. Which expression is equivalent to both \(\dfrac{-x}{y}\) and \(\dfrac{x}{-y}\)?

A. \(\dfrac{x}{y}\)   B. \(-\left(\dfrac{-x}{y}\right)\)   C. \(-1\left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)\)   D. \(\dfrac{-x}{-y}\)

3. Chase simplified the expression \(-2 \times (5 - 3)\), and found \(-2 \times 2 = 4\). Chase is incorrect. What is the correct value of the simplified expression?

4. Rashawn and Jessie are playing a game where they lose points for each incorrect answer. Rashawn answers two 3-point questions incorrectly, and Jessie answers four 2-point questions incorrectly. If neither player has answered a question correctly, who is ahead? Explain.

A. Jessie is ahead with \(-8\) points.
B. Jessie is ahead with \(-4\) points.
C. Rashawn is ahead with \(-6\) points.
D. Rashawn is ahead with 6 points.

5. Select all the expressions that are equal to \(-8\).

\(\dfrac{80}{100}\) · \(\dfrac{-800}{100}\) · \(80 \div (-10)\) · \(-80 \div (-10)\) · \(\dfrac{-80}{100}\)
