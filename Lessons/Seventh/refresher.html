Integer Word Problem Keywords

Integer word problems hide two kinds of clues: which sign a number should have, and which operation to use.

Copy Into Math Notebook - Sign Clue Words
SignClue Words
Positive (+)gained, rose, increased, above, up, climbed, deposit, income, profit, forward
Negative (−)lost, dropped, decreased, below, down, withdrawal, owe, debt, expense, below zero
Copy Into Math Notebook - Operation Clue Words
OperationClue WordsExample
Additioncombined, total change, net result, altogetherdeposit $40, then withdrawal $25
Subtractiondifference between, how much higher/colder/fartherdifference between the peak and valley elevation
Multiplicationsame change repeated, each day for __ days, loses __ every __drops $3 each day for 4 days
Divisionaverage change per unit, spread evenly over timetotal change per day

Try It

1. “The temperature dropped 6° then rose 4°.” What are the two signed numbers? What operation combines them?

2. “A stock loses $3 every day for 5 days.” Is the daily change positive or negative? What operation models the total?

3. “Find the difference between a cliff at 180 m and a lake bed at −40 m.” Which operation does “difference between” signal?

1. −6 and +4 → add them: \((-6) + 4 = -2^\circ\) total change.
2. Daily change is negative (−$3) → multiply: \((-3) \times 5 = -\$15\) total loss.
3. “Difference between” → subtract: \(180 - (-40) = 220\) m apart.
The Number Line & Absolute Value
Copy Into Math Notebook
  • Positive values sit to the right of zero; negative values sit to the left of zero.
  • Moving right increases value; moving left decreases value.
  • Absolute value \(|a|\) is the distance a number is from zero — always 0 or positive.
  • On a number line, whichever number sits further right has the greater value.

Try It: Number Line Moves

1. You start at 9 on the number line and move 6 units to the left. What number do you land on?

2. You start at 2 and move 5 units right, then 3 units left. Where do you end up?

1. Land on 3 (\(9 - 6 = 3\))
2. Land on 4 (\(2 + 5 = 7\), then \(7 - 3 = 4\))

Try It: Absolute Value & Comparing

1. Evaluate \(|-18|\).

2. Evaluate \(|-12.5| + |4|\).

3. Insert \(>\), \(<\), or \(=\): \(\frac{1}{2}\) ___ \(0.55\)

1. 18
2. 16.5 (\(12.5 + 4 = 16.5\))
3. \(<\) (because \(\frac{1}{2} = 0.50\), and \(0.50 < 0.55\))
The Coordinate Plane
Copy Into Math Notebook

A coordinate plane is formed by two perpendicular number lines meeting at the origin \((0,0)\): the x-axis (horizontal) and y-axis (vertical). It is divided into 4 quadrants.

Quadrant I: \((+,+)\) · Quadrant II: \((-,+)\) · Quadrant III: \((-,-)\) · Quadrant IV: \((+,-)\)

Try It

1. Which quadrant is the point \((-4, 5)\) in?

2. Which quadrant is the point \((6, -2)\) in?

3. State the directions to plot \((-5, -2)\) starting from the origin.

1. Quadrant II (x negative, y positive)
2. Quadrant IV (x positive, y negative)
3. Move 5 units left, then 2 units down.
Decimals & Fraction Division
Copy Into Math Notebook

Fraction division (“keep, change, flip”): \(\dfrac{a}{b} \div \dfrac{c}{d} = \dfrac{a}{b} \times \dfrac{d}{c}\)

Try It

1. Solve \(\frac{2}{5} \div \frac{1}{3}\)

2. Solve \(14.50 - 6.25\)

1. \(\frac{6}{5}\) or \(1\frac{1}{5}\) (\(\frac{2}{5} \times \frac{3}{1} = \frac{6}{5}\))
2. 8.25
Exponents & Algebraic Expressions
Copy Into Math Notebook

An exponent shows repeated multiplication: \(a^n = \underbrace{a \times a \times \cdots \times a}_{n \text{ times}}\). Note: \(3^2 = 3 \times 3 = 9\) (not \(3 \times 2 = 6\)).

Terms with the same variable and exponent can be combined by adding or subtracting their coefficients.

Try It

1. Evaluate \(5^3\).

2. Evaluate \(3y - 4\) when \(y = 6\).

3. Simplify \(8m + 3m - 2m\).

1. 125 (\(5 \times 5 \times 5\))
2. 14 (\(3(6) - 4 = 18 - 4\))
3. \(9m\) (\(8 + 3 - 2 = 9\))
Unit Rates & Percentages
Copy Into Math Notebook

Unit rate: a ratio comparing a quantity to 1 single unit.

Percent of a number: convert the percent to a decimal, then multiply (\(P\% \times \text{total}\)).

Try It

1. A pack of 4 notebooks costs $12.00. What is the cost per notebook?

2. What is 15% of 60?

1. $3.00 per notebook (\(12 \div 4\))
2. 9 (\(0.15 \times 60\))
Perimeter, Area & Volume Foundations
Copy Into Math Notebook

Rectangle area: \(A = l \times w\) · Triangle area: \(A = \frac{1}{2} \times b \times h\)

Volume of a rectangular prism: \(V = l \times w \times h\)

Try It

1. Calculate the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 5 cm.

2. A storage box measures 6 ft long, 3 ft wide, and 4 ft high. What is its volume?

1. 40 cm² (\(8 \times 5\))
2. 72 ft³ (\(6 \times 3 \times 4\))

Preview Challenge: Opposite Numbers

Opposite numbers sit the same distance from zero on a number line, but on opposite sides. The opposite of \(+7\) is \(-7\); the opposite of \(-12\) is \(+12\).

What result do you always get when you add any number to its exact opposite? Example: \(8 + (-8) = {?}\)

Always zero (0)! This is called the Additive Inverse Property, and it's the foundation for integer arithmetic in our next topic.
