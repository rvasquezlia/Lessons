Finding a Square or a Cube
The Rules
  • Square: \(n^2 = n \times n\).
  • Cube: \(n^3 = n \times n \times n\).

Finding a Square Root or a Cube Root
The Rules
  • Square root: \(\sqrt{n}\) asks "what number times itself equals \(n\)?"
  • Cube root: \(\sqrt[3]{n}\) asks "what number, used three times as a factor, equals \(n\)?"
  • Recognizing a perfect square or perfect cube from the reference chart is the fastest way to answer either question.

Real-World Applications
The Connection

A square's area is its side length squared, so working backward from area to side length is a square root. A cube's volume is its edge length cubed, so working backward from volume to edge length is a cube root.
