Key Vocabulary: Squares, Cubes & Roots
Every term below shows up in this topic. Read through them once, then come back any time a word trips you up.
Base

The number being multiplied by itself in a square or cube.

In \(7^2\), the base is 7
Square (\(n^2\))

A number multiplied by itself one time.

\(6^2 = 36\)
Cube (\(n^3\))

A number multiplied by itself, then by itself again (three factors total).

\(4^3 = 64\)
Perfect Square

A number that is the square of a whole number.

36 is a perfect square
Perfect Cube

A number that is the cube of a whole number.

64 is a perfect cube
Radical

The root symbol (\(\sqrt{\phantom{x}}\) or \(\sqrt[3]{\phantom{x}}\)), and any expression that uses it.

\(\sqrt{49}\) is a radical expression
Radicand

The number underneath the radical symbol - the number a root is being taken of.

In \(\sqrt{81}\), the radicand is 81
Square Root (\(\sqrt{n}\))

The "undo" of squaring: what number times itself equals \(n\)?

\(\sqrt{36} = 6\)
Cube Root (\(\sqrt[3]{n}\))

The "undo" of cubing: what number, used as a factor three times, equals \(n\)?

\(\sqrt[3]{64} = 4\)
Index

The small number tucked into the radical's checkmark showing which root to take - 2 (square root) is usually left off.

The 3 in \(\sqrt[3]{64}\) is the index
