1. Squares & Cubes
  • Square: \(n^2 = n \times n\). Example: \(9^2 = 81\).
  • Cube: \(n^3 = n \times n \times n\). Example: \(5^3 = 125\).
2. Square Roots & Cube Roots

A root undoes a power. \(\sqrt{n}\) asks "what times itself gives \(n\)?" \(\sqrt[3]{n}\) asks "what times itself three times gives \(n\)?"

Common Misconceptions to Watch For
  • Confusing squaring (\(n \times n\)) with doubling (\(n \times 2\)).
  • Confusing a cube (three factors) with a square (two factors).
  • Dividing by the root's index instead of finding the actual root (e.g. thinking \(\sqrt{64} = 32\)).
