Word Problems

Standards: 8.EE.A.2

How this page works Every item is a real-world situation - decide whether you need a square, a cube, a square root, or a cube root, then solve. Calculators are not allowed.
Area & Square Roots
A square's area equals its side length squared - so working backward from an area to a side length means taking a square root.
Volume & Cube Roots
A cube's volume equals its edge length cubed - so working backward from a volume to an edge length means taking a cube root.
Mixed & Challenge Problems
These combine squares, cubes, and roots in one problem, or ask you to reason about how they scale.
