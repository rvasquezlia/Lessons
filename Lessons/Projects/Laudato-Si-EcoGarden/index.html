Laudato Si' Eco-Garden

How can we design a garden that honors God's creation while staying within our $100.00 budget using decimal calculations?

How this page works
  • This is your digital version of the paper worksheet - same math, same 2-day plan, but the garden items, calculations, and layout are all interactive here.
  • Drag items from the Store Catalog into your Ledger (or tap "Add to Cart"). Type your rounded estimate and your exact Line Total, then hit Check - you get 2 tries before the correct math is revealed and that row locks.
  • On Day 2 you'll drag your purchased items onto a 6×6 garden grid and can flip to a real-life preview of your design.
  • When you're finished, type your name above and click Print My Progress to submit proof of your work.
