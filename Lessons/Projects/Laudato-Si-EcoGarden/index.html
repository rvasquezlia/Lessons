How this page works
  • This is your digital version of the paper worksheet - same math, same 2-day plan, but the garden items, calculations, and layout are all interactive here.
  • Drag items from the Store Catalog into your Ledger (or tap "Add to Cart"). Rounded Estimate and Line Total are checked separately - unlimited tries, but every attempt is counted and shown on your printed report, and each field locks as soon as you get it right.
  • On Day 2 you'll drag your purchased items onto a 6×6 garden grid and can flip to a real-life preview of your design.
  • Type your team code above and click Start / Resume - it remembers your names and everything you've done so far, so you can pick up right where you left off.
  • When you're finished, click Print My Progress, or scroll to the bottom to download your completion badge and your garden sign.
