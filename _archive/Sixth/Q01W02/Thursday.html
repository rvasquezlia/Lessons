Decimal Jigsaw: Real-World Precision

Every group is solving the same kind of decimal problems - but set in a different real job. Medicine, market/business, engineering, and the arts all depend on getting decimals right. Solve your group's 6 problems together on your physical cards, then type and submit each answer below so it saves to your progress.
Guided Practice

Before you pick your group, warm up with 5 quick problems: solve each on paper in your notebook first, then type your answer and check it. You get up to 2 tries per problem - after a second miss, the correct method is shown and it locks.

Step 1

Which group are you in today?

Pick the same group your teacher assigned to your table. Once you confirm, it locks in for the rest of the lesson.

For this problem Solve each problem on your physical card with your group first, then type your own answer in each box below and click Submit right after solving it. All 6 are real scenarios from your group's field - no bare computation drills.
Mixing Time: Share What You Learned
What to do on this tab Once your table finishes all 6 problems, you'll have about 15 minutes to mix: get up, move around the room, and find someone from a different group. Ask them what their problems were about and why decimal precision matters in their field. Then come back here, pick which group you visited, and write 2-3 sentences about what you learned.
Pick your group on Tab 1 first - once you do, this tab will remind you which other groups you can visit.
Group Sharing Rotation

You are in the group. Visit a table from a different group and ask them to explain one of their problems and why precision matters in their field.
