Q01W02 - Extra Resources

Challenge Bank

For the teacher Print and hand out to early finishers or as homework, any day this week. No solutions are shown here (they stay in the Teacher Guide's Challenge Bank tab).

Tip: in the print dialog, set Margins to "Default" and turn off "Headers and footers" for the cleanest printout.

Monday - Direct Instruction, Harder Tier

C1. Solve \(3(2x - 5) - 4 = 2(x + 7)\).

C2. Solve \(-(x - 6) = 2x + 3\).

C3. Classify \(4(x + 3) = 2(2x + 6)\). Is this always, sometimes, or never true?

C4. Solve \(5(2x + 1) - 7 = 3x - 30\).

C5. Solve \(-2(x + 4) = 3x + 7\).

Tuesday - Practice, Harder Tier

C1. Solve \(2(3x - 1) + 5 = 3(x + 4) - x\).

C2. Solve \(5x - 2(x - 3) = 2x + 20\).

C3. Classify \(6x + 11 = 6x - 4\). Explain what the result tells you.

C4. Classify \(4(x - 3) + 2x = 6(x - 2)\).

C5. Solve \(7x - 2(x + 1) = 3x + 8\).

Wednesday - Classification, Harder Tier

C1. Classify \(3(x + 2) - x = 2x + 6\).

C2. Classify \(4x + 9 = 4x - 2\).

C3. Solve \(7 - 2(x - 3) = x + 1\).

C4. Classify \(6x - 1 = 2(3x + 4)\).

C5. Classify \(9 - 3(x - 1) = 15 - 3x\).

Thursday - Word Problems, Harder Tier

C1. Two consecutive integers have a sum of 47. Write and solve an equation to find both integers.

C2. Solve \(2(4x - 3) = 3(2x + 1) + 1\).

C3. Three consecutive even integers have a sum of 72. Write and solve an equation to find all three.

C4. Two numbers differ by 12 and have a sum of 58. Write and solve an equation to find both numbers.

C5. Solve \(4(x - 2) + 3 = 2x + 9\).

Friday - Enrichment for Early Finishers

C1. Classify \(5(x + 2) - 3x = 2x + 10\).

C2. Solve \(9 - 4(x - 2) = 2x - 7\).

C3. Solve \(6(x - 1) - 2x = 3x + 2\).

C4. A parent is 4 times as old as her son; in 6 years she will be 3 times as old as her son will be then. Write and solve an equation for the son's current age, \(x\).

C5. Classify \(5x - 3(x - 2) = 2x + 6\).
